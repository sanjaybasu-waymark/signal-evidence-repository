## Content Quality Assessment

All recommendations in the library meet the following quality criteria:

- ✅ Evidence-based with peer-reviewed citations
- ✅ Structured with clear recommendation, rationale, and expected outcomes
- ✅ Role-specific guidance tailored to professional responsibilities
- ✅ Implementation notes for practical application
- ✅ Evidence levels and citation tracking

## PRD Alignment Summary

The content coverage successfully aligns with the PRD requirements:

1. **Domain Coverage**: All major clinical and social domains specified in the PRD are represented with at least one high-quality recommendation.
2. **Role-Based Organization**: Content is organized by professional role, with all key roles represented.
3. **Evidence-Based Approach**: All recommendations include evidence levels and citations to peer-reviewed literature.
4. **Structured Format**: Recommendations follow a consistent structure with recommendation, rationale, expected outcomes, and implementation notes.

## Areas for Future Enhancement

While the current content provides comprehensive coverage of the domains and roles specified in the PRD, several areas could be enhanced in future iterations:

1. **Additional Recommendations**: Expand to include multiple recommendations per domain for more comprehensive coverage.
2. **Subdomain Specificity**: Add more granular subdomain categorization within major domains.
3. **Interaction Effects**: Develop recommendations addressing comorbidities and interaction effects between conditions.
4. **Localization**: Adapt recommendations for specific geographic regions or populations.

## Conclusion

The Signal Evidence Library content successfully covers all domains and roles specified in the PRD, with high-quality, evidence-based recommendations structured for effective retrieval and application. The library provides a solid foundation for the RAG database that will support the opportunity/next best action identification tool.
