Quality Scoring Methodology

Protocol quality scores (0-10) are based on: Study Design (RCTs=highest, systematic reviews, cohort studies), Sample Size (larger samples=higher scores), Population Relevance (Medicaid/vulnerable populations), Implementation Detail (specific, actionable protocols), and Outcome Measurement (validated, clinically meaningful metrics). Scores ≥8.0 indicate high-quality evidence suitable for immediate implementation.
