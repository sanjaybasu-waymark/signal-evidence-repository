# Transportation Insecurity Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 14
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/c3a72c90-0119-427e-aaf5-0dcb9f06d60a

### Screening and Identification

1. **Routine Standardized Assessment**
   - Integrate social determinants of health (SDOH) screening into clinical workflows
   - Leverage electronic health record for documentation
   - Maintain up-to-date list of community resources
   - Citation: American Heart Association recommendations

2. **Validated Screening Tools**
   - Implement Accountable Health Communities (AHC) 10-item tool
   - Consider Transportation Security Index (16-item) for more granular assessment
   - Use two-step approach: brief universal screener followed by detailed assessment if positive
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

### Direct Transportation Support

1. **Public Transportation Access**
   - Provide bus passes to facilitate access to medical appointments
   - Target populations with identified transportation barriers
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

2. **Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT)**
   - Implement NEMT services for eligible patients
   - Tailor to needs of specific populations (older adults, mobility challenges)
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

3. **Ridesharing Partnerships**
   - Establish partnerships with ridesharing companies (e.g., Uber, Lyft)
   - Provide vouchers for medical appointments
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

4. **Free Shuttle Services**
   - Operate shuttle services between residential areas and healthcare facilities
   - Target areas with high concentration of transportation-insecure patients
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

### Integrated Care Management

1. **Care Coordination**
   - Combine transportation support with comprehensive care coordination
   - Address both transportation and other social determinants of health
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

2. **Patient Navigation**
   - Train navigators to identify and address transportation barriers
   - Connect patients to appropriate transportation resources
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

3. **Multicomponent Interventions**
   - Implement interventions addressing multiple barriers simultaneously
   - Include counseling, education, and assistance from community health workers or nurses
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

### Technology-Enabled Solutions

1. **Mobile Applications**
   - Implement mobile applications for ride booking
   - Integrate with healthcare appointment systems
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

2. **Telehealth Services**
   - Expand telehealth to reduce need for in-person visits
   - Ensure accessibility for patients with limited technology access
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

3. **Community-Based Care Sites**
   - Establish care sites in community locations (e.g., federally qualified health centers)
   - Bring care closer to patients with transportation barriers
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators and Nurse Care Managers**
   - Screen for transportation barriers using validated tools
   - Integrate positive screens into care plans
   - Arrange transportation services (NEMT, ride-share vouchers, bus passes)
   - Follow up to confirm service utilization
   - Reassess needs at subsequent encounters
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

2. **Community Health Workers**
   - Conduct home visits to assess transportation needs
   - Connect patients to community transportation resources
   - Provide education on available transportation options
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

3. **Social Workers**
   - Address transportation barriers within broader social needs assessment
   - Connect patients to financial assistance for transportation
   - Advocate for policy changes to improve transportation access
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

### Implementation Considerations

1. **Sustainable Funding**
   - Develop diverse funding streams (philanthropy, grants, internal health system dollars)
   - Advocate for insurance reimbursement for transportation services
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research

2. **Scalable Approaches**
   - Design interventions that can be scaled to larger populations
   - Leverage technology and community partnerships
   - Citation: American Heart Association recommendations

3. **Addressing Logistical Barriers**
   - Streamline eligibility for existing programs
   - Reduce administrative complexity
   - Ensure reliable, flexible transportation options
   - Citation: Transportation insecurity research
