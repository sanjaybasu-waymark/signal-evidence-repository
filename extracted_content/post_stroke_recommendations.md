# Post-Stroke Care Management Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 7
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/305ea9b3-9ad4-4136-9ace-6d1697e4ccdf

### Multidisciplinary and Integrated Care Models

1. **Team-Based Approaches**
   - Implement multidisciplinary teams including physicians, nurses, pharmacists, social workers, and rehabilitation specialists
   - Focus on risk factor control and secondary prevention
   - Establish regular hospital and outpatient programs for adherence and risk factor monitoring
   - Citation: 2021 Guideline for the Prevention of Stroke in Patients With Stroke and Transient Ischemic Attack

2. **Case Management-Based Programs**
   - Implement the SOS-Care model with home and phone consultations
   - Provide education and individualized care plans
   - Target significant reductions in stroke recurrence and vascular death
   - Improve achievement of treatment goals for hypertension, dyslipidemia, diabetes, BMI, and medication adherence
   - Citation: Case Management-Based Post-Stroke Care for Patients With Acute Stroke and TIA (SOS-Care): Prospective Cohort Study

3. **Integrated Care Pathway (ABC Pathway)**
   - Implement the European Society of Cardiology Council on Stroke's ABC pathway:
     - **A**ppropriate antithrombotic therapy
     - **B**etter functional and psychological status
     - **C**ardiovascular risk factor and comorbidity optimization
   - Use as a holistic framework for post-stroke management
   - Citation: Integrated Care for Optimizing the Management of Stroke and Associated Heart Disease: Position Paper of the European Society of Cardiology Council on Stroke

### Risk Factor Management and Secondary Prevention

1. **Hypertension Control**
   - Target blood pressure <130/80 mm Hg for most patients after ischemic stroke
   - Follow American Heart Association/American Stroke Association recommendations
   - Implement nurse- and pharmacist-led interventions for blood pressure management
   - Citation: 2021 Guideline for the Prevention of Stroke in Patients With Stroke and Transient Ischemic Attack

2. **Medication Management**
   - Prescribe statin therapy as indicated
   - Provide antiplatelet or anticoagulation therapy as appropriate
   - Monitor adherence to medication regimens
   - Citation: Primary Care of Adult Patients After Stroke: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association/American Stroke Association

3. **Diabetes Management**
   - Implement structured diabetes care for post-stroke patients
   - Monitor glycemic control regularly
   - Coordinate with endocrinology when needed
   - Citation: 2021 Guideline for the Prevention of Stroke in Patients With Stroke and Transient Ischemic Attack

4. **Lifestyle Modification**
   - Provide comprehensive support for diet improvements
   - Encourage regular exercise appropriate to functional status
   - Implement smoking cessation programs
   - Citation: Primary Care of Adult Patients After Stroke: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association/American Stroke Association

5. **Adherence Monitoring**
   - Establish systems to track medication adherence
   - Address social determinants of health affecting adherence
   - Citation: Case Management-Based Post-Stroke Care for Patients With Acute Stroke and TIA (SOS-Care)

### Rehabilitation and Functional Recovery

1. **Early, Structured Rehabilitation**
   - Begin rehabilitation as soon as medically feasible
   - Implement multimodal approaches including physical, occupational, and speech therapy
   - Focus on improving function, independence, and quality of life
   - Citation: Primary Care of Adult Patients After Stroke: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association/American Stroke Association

2. **Validated Screening Tools**
   - Use validated instruments to assess mobility, cognition, and depression
   - Ensure prompt linkage to appropriate resources based on screening results
   - Citation: 2021 Guideline for the Prevention of Stroke in Patients With Stroke and Transient Ischemic Attack

3. **Community and Home-Based Rehabilitation**
   - Implement telerehabilitation and home-based programs
   - Achieve functional improvements comparable to facility-based programs
   - Increase access and engagement for patients with mobility or transportation limitations
   - Citation: Integrated Care for Optimizing the Management of Stroke and Associated Heart Disease

### Patient and Caregiver Support

1. **Education and Engagement**
   - Provide structured education on stroke prevention, recovery, and management
   - Engage patients in self-management strategies
   - Include caregivers in education and training
   - Citation: Primary Care of Adult Patients After Stroke: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association/American Stroke Association

2. **Psychosocial Support**
   - Screen for and address depression, anxiety, and other psychological impacts
   - Provide access to support groups and counseling
   - Citation: Integrated Care for Optimizing the Management of Stroke and Associated Heart Disease

3. **Social Work Involvement**
   - Integrate social work services to address social determinants of health
   - Develop community resource networks
   - Facilitate transitions of care
   - Support both patients and caregivers
   - Citation: Case Management-Based Post-Stroke Care for Patients With Acute Stroke and TIA (SOS-Care)

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators**
   - Facilitate communication between multidisciplinary team members
   - Track patient progress through care continuum
   - Ensure follow-up appointments are scheduled and attended
   - Citation: Primary Care of Adult Patients After Stroke: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association/American Stroke Association

2. **Nurse Care Managers**
   - Lead blood pressure management interventions
   - Provide patient education and self-management support
   - Conduct regular follow-up via home visits or phone consultations
   - Citation: Case Management-Based Post-Stroke Care for Patients With Acute Stroke and TIA (SOS-Care)

3. **Pharmacists**
   - Lead lipid management interventions
   - Conduct medication reconciliation and adherence counseling
   - Monitor for drug interactions and side effects
   - Citation: 2021 Guideline for the Prevention of Stroke in Patients With Stroke and Transient Ischemic Attack

4. **Social Workers**
   - Address social determinants affecting recovery and secondary prevention
   - Connect patients to community resources
   - Support caregivers and family members
   - Citation: Primary Care of Adult Patients After Stroke: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association/American Stroke Association

5. **Community Health Workers**
   - Bridge gaps between clinical and community settings
   - Provide culturally appropriate education and support
   - Assist with navigation of healthcare and social service systems
   - Citation: Integrated Care for Optimizing the Management of Stroke and Associated Heart Disease
