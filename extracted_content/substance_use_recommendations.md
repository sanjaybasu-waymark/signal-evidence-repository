# Substance Use Disorders Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 17
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/c853bfdf-9bed-47dc-8d5b-19b1c8e61938

### Screening and Identification

1. **Routine Screening**
   - Implement routine screening for unhealthy drug use in primary care
   - Use validated tools (e.g., single-question screens, DAST, AUDIT)
   - Integrate as foundational population health strategy
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

2. **SBIRT Model**
   - Implement Screening, Brief Intervention, and Referral to Treatment
   - Integrate brief motivational counseling
   - Establish streamlined referral pathways
   - Incorporate into routine care
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

### Care Management Interventions

1. **Case Management**
   - Conduct assessment, care planning, service linkage, and ongoing monitoring
   - Target improved treatment linkage and retention
   - Facilitate entry into treatment programs
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

2. **Chronic Care Management**
   - Incorporate motivational interviewing
   - Implement relapse prevention strategies
   - Coordinate medical/psychiatric care
   - Target improved abstinence for specific subgroups (e.g., alcohol dependence)
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

3. **Continuing Care**
   - Provide longitudinal, adaptive follow-up
   - Conduct regular assessment and treatment adjustment
   - Target improved outcomes for high-risk patients
   - Enhance with digital/mobile health interventions
   - Implement incentives for engagement
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

### Behavioral and Psychosocial Interventions

1. **Contingency Management**
   - Use tangible incentives contingent on objective evidence of behavior change
   - Implement for stimulant use disorders (cocaine, methamphetamine)
   - Maximize efficacy with higher-value incentives
   - Minimize delay between behavior and reward
   - Extend intervention duration
   - Citation: VA/DoD recommendations

2. **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy**
   - Implement as evidence-based behavioral therapy
   - Target substance use reduction
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

3. **Motivational Enhancement Therapy**
   - Implement as evidence-based behavioral therapy
   - Target improved treatment engagement
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

4. **12-Step Facilitation**
   - Implement as evidence-based behavioral therapy
   - Connect patients to community support
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

5. **Community Reinforcement**
   - Implement as evidence-based behavioral therapy
   - Target improved social functioning
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

6. **Mutual Help Group Involvement**
   - Facilitate participation in peer support groups
   - Target sustained recovery
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

### Integrated and Primary Care-Based Models

1. **Multidisciplinary, Coordinated Care Models**
   - Implement for opioid use disorder
   - Integrate medication-assisted treatment (MAT) with buprenorphine or methadone
   - Deliver in primary care settings
   - Target outcomes equivalent to specialty addiction treatment
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

2. **Integrated Clinical Teams**
   - Form teams with diverse expertise
   - Include care managers (nurses or pharmacists)
   - Develop patient agreements
   - Implement flexible induction protocols
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators**
   - Coordinate multidisciplinary care
   - Facilitate referrals to SUD treatment
   - Ensure follow-up after transitions
   - Monitor engagement and retention
   - Address barriers (transportation, housing)
   - Provide ongoing outreach
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

2. **Community Health Workers**
   - Deliver psychoeducation
   - Provide motivational enhancement
   - Offer personalized feedback
   - Support engagement through outreach and navigation
   - Address social determinants of health
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

3. **Pharmacists**
   - Provide medication education
   - Support harm reduction (naloxone distribution)
   - Facilitate medication for opioid use disorder (MOUD) access
   - Collaborate with prescribers for medication induction and maintenance
   - Monitor adherence
   - Manage drug interactions
   - Serve as accessible points for patient counseling
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

4. **Pharmacy Technicians**
   - Serve as frontline contacts in community pharmacies
   - Identify patients at risk
   - Provide nonjudgmental support
   - Facilitate communication between patients and pharmacists
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research

5. **Nurse Care Managers**
   - Lead care management for MOUD in primary care
   - Conduct patient assessments
   - Coordinate with prescribers
   - Monitor treatment adherence
   - Provide ongoing support
   - Citation: Massachusetts model research

6. **Social Workers**
   - Conduct SUD assessment
   - Provide case management
   - Connect patients to social and behavioral health resources
   - Assist with insurance and benefits
   - Advocate for integrated care
   - Address psychosocial needs
   - Support structural changes to improve access
   - Citation: Substance use disorder research
