# Depression Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 19
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/3c2c0b7f-1d81-46ed-a849-da20c02ff080

### First-Line Individual Interventions

1. **Psychotherapies**
   - Implement cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)
   - Offer behavioral activation
   - Provide interpersonal therapy
   - Deliver mindfulness-based therapy
   - Implement problem-solving therapy
   - Target medium to large effect sizes (SMDs 0.50 to 0.73)
   - Citation: Depression management research

2. **Pharmacologic Treatments**
   - Prescribe selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs)
   - Consider serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitors (SNRIs)
   - Target small to medium effect sizes (SMDs 0.23-0.48)
   - Individualize based on side effect profiles, comorbidities, and patient preference
   - Citation: Depression management research

3. **Combined Approach**
   - Implement combined psychotherapy and pharmacotherapy for severe or chronic depression
   - Target greater symptom improvement than either modality alone
   - Citation: Depression management research

### Care Management and Population Health Interventions

1. **Collaborative Care Models**
   - Integrate care managers (nurses or social workers)
   - Provide systematic follow-up
   - Implement measurement-based care (e.g., regular PHQ-9 assessments)
   - Deliver patient education
   - Incorporate behavioral activation
   - Coordinate with primary care and psychiatric consultants
   - Target improved depression symptom remission, treatment response, and adherence
   - Reduce time to remission (hazard ratio 2.48, median time 86 vs 614 days)
   - Improve quality of life, functional status, and patient satisfaction
   - Citation: Depression management research

2. **Remote and Centralized Care Management**
   - Implement population-based programs using remote monitoring and outreach
   - Target large health systems
   - Ensure follow-up after treatment initiation
   - Provide outreach to high-risk patients
   - Citation: Depression management research

### Implementation Considerations

1. **Shared Decision-Making**
   - Incorporate patient preferences in treatment decisions
   - Ensure culturally and linguistically appropriate care
   - Citation: Depression management research

2. **Structured Assessment**
   - Assess depression severity systematically
   - Use validated screening and monitoring tools
   - Citation: Depression management research

3. **Ongoing Monitoring**
   - Track symptoms and response over time
   - Use measurement-based care approaches
   - Citation: Depression management research

4. **Stepped Care Approaches**
   - Escalate treatment intensity based on response
   - Adjust treatment approach when needed
   - Citation: Depression management research

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Primary Care Providers**
   - Screen for depression using validated tools
   - Initiate first-line treatments
   - Collaborate with mental health specialists
   - Citation: Depression management research

2. **Care Managers**
   - Provide systematic follow-up
   - Conduct regular symptom assessments
   - Coordinate between primary care and mental health
   - Deliver patient education and behavioral activation
   - Citation: Depression management research

3. **Mental Health Specialists**
   - Provide consultation to primary care
   - Deliver evidence-based psychotherapies
   - Manage complex cases
   - Citation: Depression management research

4. **Psychiatrists**
   - Provide medication management for complex cases
   - Consult on treatment-resistant depression
   - Support primary care in medication selection
   - Citation: Depression management research
