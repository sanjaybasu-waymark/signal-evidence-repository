# Asthma Management Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 22
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/cd27694a-a2a8-408b-8bc2-b077a2ee7a64

### Role-Specific Recommendations

#### Care Coordinators

1. **Care Coordination and Integration**
   - Facilitate seamless communication and integration of asthma management across home, school, community, and healthcare settings
   - Facilitate the development and implementation of personalized asthma action plans (AAPs)
   - Ensure timely communication between primary care providers and specialists
   - Coordinate access to Medicaid-funded programs and social services for eligible patients
   - Citation: Ensuring Equitable Access to Guideline-Based Asthma Care Across the Lifespan

2. **Barrier Management**
   - Address logistical barriers such as transportation and scheduling
   - Improve adherence to asthma care plans
   - Citation: Ensuring Equitable Access to Guideline-Based Asthma Care Across the Lifespan

#### Nurse Care Managers

1. **Clinical Monitoring and Education**
   - Provide regular follow-ups to monitor asthma control using validated tools like the Asthma Control Test (ACT) or Asthma Control Questionnaire (ACQ)
   - Educate patients on proper inhaler techniques
   - Promote adherence to controller medications
   - Teach strategies for avoiding asthma triggers
   - Citation: Self-Management Interventions to Reduce Healthcare Use and Improve Quality of Life Among Patients With Asthma

2. **Allergen Management**
   - Assist in implementing multicomponent allergen-specific mitigation measures for patients with allergen-triggered asthma
   - Citation: Systematic Meta-Review of Supported Self-Management for Asthma

#### Community Health Workers (CHWs)

1. **Environmental Assessment and Education**
   - Conduct home visits to assess environmental triggers (dust mites, cockroaches, mold, pet dander)
   - Provide tailored education on asthma management
   - Teach families how to reduce exposure to triggers and improve indoor air quality
   - Citation: Role of Community Health Workers in Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus Self-Management

2. **Resource Connection and Advocacy**
   - Connect families to community resources such as housing improvement programs
   - Advocate for structural changes to address substandard housing conditions that exacerbate asthma
   - Citation: Ensuring Equitable Access to Guideline-Based Asthma Care Across the Lifespan

3. **Social Determinants Support**
   - Help families overcome barriers such as transportation challenges, food insecurity, and limited access to healthcare
   - Facilitate communication between families, schools, and healthcare providers
   - Ensure continuity of care, including the transfer of asthma medications and administration forms to school nurses for children with asthma
   - Citation: Ensuring Equitable Access to Guideline-Based Asthma Care Across the Lifespan

#### Pharmacists

1. **Medication Management and Education**
   - Conduct comprehensive medication reviews
   - Provide education on proper inhaler technique
   - Monitor medication adherence
   - Report concerns to primary care providers to optimize therapy
   - Citation: Self-Management Interventions to Reduce Healthcare Use and Improve Quality of Life Among Patients With Asthma

2. **Intervention Implementation**
   - Lead pharmacist-led interventions to improve asthma control, quality of life, and inhaler technique
   - Citation: Systematic Meta-Review of Supported Self-Management for Asthma

#### Pharmacy Technicians

1. **Adherence Support**
   - Identify patients with poor medication adherence
   - Help streamline prescription refills to ensure continuity of therapy
   - Support pharmacists in delivering educational interventions
   - Citation: Ensuring Equitable Access to Guideline-Based Asthma Care Across the Lifespan

#### Social Workers

1. **Social Services Access**
   - Assist families in accessing Medicaid-funded programs and other social services
   - Address barriers to asthma care, such as financial constraints and lack of transportation
   - Advocate for housing improvements and environmental control measures
   - Citation: Ensuring Equitable Access to Guideline-Based Asthma Care Across the Lifespan

2. **Collaborative Care**
   - Collaborate with CHWs and care coordinators to address broader social determinants of health
   - Address food insecurity and employment challenges that may indirectly impact asthma outcomes
   - Citation: Ensuring Equitable Access to Guideline-Based Asthma Care Across the Lifespan

### General Recommendations for Asthma Management

1. **Supported Self-Management**
   - Implement patient education, personalized AAPs, and regular professional reviews
   - Provide structured reviews totaling at least two hours
   - Implement multidisciplinary case management for patients with complex disease
   - Citation: Self-Management Interventions to Reduce Healthcare Use and Improve Quality of Life Among Patients With Asthma

2. **Community-Based Interventions**
   - Deploy mobile care units such as the Breathmobile program to address social determinants of health
   - Deliver care directly to underserved communities
   - Citation: Ensuring Equitable Access to Guideline-Based Asthma Care Across the Lifespan

3. **Technology and Digital Tools**
   - Implement telehealth and digital tools, including remote monitoring systems and digital inhalers
   - Integrate personalized AAPs with digital platforms for tailored interventions and real-time feedback
   - Focus on patients with limited access to in-person care
   - Citation: Questions in Mild Asthma: An Official American Thoracic Society Research Statement

4. **Non-Pharmacologic Interventions**
   - Promote aerobic exercise programs to enhance lung function and asthma control
   - Encourage breathing exercises, including yoga, Buteyko breathing, and diaphragmatic retraining
   - Support weight loss interventions, particularly for younger patients
   - Citation: Questions in Mild Asthma: An Official American Thoracic Society Research Statement

5. **Care Coordination**
   - Implement multidisciplinary care coordination involving schools, homes, and community resources
   - Focus on children with asthma
   - Address environmental triggers, provide education, and facilitate access to care
   - Citation: Ensuring Equitable Access to Guideline-Based Asthma Care Across the Lifespan
