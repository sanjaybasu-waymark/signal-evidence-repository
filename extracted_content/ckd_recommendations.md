# Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Care Management Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 9
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/651ba9ef-8a60-40fc-bf93-dec4696d4901

### Key Principles of CKD Care Management

1. **Early Detection and Risk Stratification**
   - Use eGFR and albuminuria for screening and staging
   - Focus on high-risk groups such as those with diabetes or hypertension
   - Implement routine screening in high-risk populations
   - Citation: Practical Approach to Detection and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease for the Primary Care Clinician

2. **Individualized Follow-up**
   - Tailor monitoring intervals based on CKD stage and progression risk
   - Ensure timely nephrology referral for patients with advanced disease
   - Refer patients with eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m², rapidly progressive disease, or refractory hypertension
   - Citation: Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis and Management: A Review

### Evidence-Based Interventions

1. **Blood Pressure Control**
   - Target <130/80 mmHg to slow CKD progression and reduce cardiovascular risk
   - Individualize targets based on comorbidities and tolerability
   - Citation: Practical Approach to Detection and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease for the Primary Care Clinician

2. **Glycemic Management**
   - Maintain hemoglobin A1c ≤7% for patients with diabetes
   - Reduce risk or slow progression of CKD
   - Citation: Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis and Management: A Review

3. **Statin Therapy**
   - Indicated for cardiovascular risk reduction in most adults with CKD not on dialysis
   - Citation: Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis and Management: A Review

4. **Renin-Angiotensin System Blockade**
   - Use ACE inhibitors or ARBs for patients with albuminuria and hypertension
   - Titrate to maximally tolerated doses
   - Monitor for hyperkalemia and changes in serum creatinine
   - Citation: Practical Approach to Detection and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease for the Primary Care Clinician

5. **SGLT2 Inhibitors**
   - Recommended for patients with diabetes and CKD
   - Increasingly indicated for non-diabetic CKD
   - Reduce CKD progression and cardiovascular events
   - Citation: Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis and Management: A Review

6. **Non-steroidal Mineralocorticoid Receptor Antagonists (ns-MRAs)**
   - Finerenone indicated in select patients with diabetic CKD and albuminuria
   - Further reduces progression and cardiovascular risk
   - Citation: Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis and Management: A Review

7. **GLP-1 Receptor Agonists**
   - Consider for patients with diabetes and CKD
   - Provides additional glycemic and cardiovascular benefit
   - Citation: Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis and Management: A Review

8. **Lifestyle Interventions**
   - Recommend plant-dominant, low-protein, and low-salt diet
   - Encourage increased physical activity
   - Support smoking cessation
   - Citation: Practical Approach to Detection and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease for the Primary Care Clinician

9. **Medication Safety**
   - Avoid nephrotoxins (e.g., NSAIDs)
   - Adjust doses of renally cleared medications based on eGFR
   - Prevent iatrogenic harm
   - Citation: Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis and Management: A Review

### Population Health and Team-Based Approaches

1. **Multidisciplinary, Patient-Centered Care Teams**
   - Include dietitians, pharmacists, educators, and social workers
   - Improve care coordination, medication management, and patient education
   - Citation: Practical Approach to Detection and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease for the Primary Care Clinician

2. **Structured Education Programs**
   - Develop programs for patients and care partners
   - Promote self-management, informed decision-making, and adherence to therapy
   - Citation: Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis and Management: A Review

3. **Telehealth and Digital Health Tools**
   - Implement telehealth services for remote monitoring and consultation
   - Use EHR-based population health management tools
   - Develop and maintain CKD registries
   - Facilitate early identification of high-risk patients
   - Enable timely nephrology input
   - Support coordinated care delivery
   - Citation: Practical Approach to Detection and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease for the Primary Care Clinician

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators and Nurse Care Managers**
   - Identify high-risk CKD patients using EHR-based registries and risk stratification tools
   - Develop and update individualized care plans
   - Ensure proactive follow-up (blood pressure, glycemic control, laboratory parameters)
   - Facilitate timely nephrology referrals
   - Coordinate communication among team members
   - Provide structured education and self-management support
   - Citation: Practical Approach to Detection and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease for the Primary Care Clinician

2. **Community Health Workers**
   - Build trust with patients
   - Deliver culturally tailored education
   - Provide care navigation, particularly for underserved populations
   - Address social determinants of health
   - Support medication adherence
   - Empower patients to engage in self-management
   - Connect patients to community resources
   - Citation: Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis and Management: A Review

3. **Pharmacists and Pharmacy Technicians**
   - Perform medication reconciliation
   - Identify and mitigate nephrotoxic drugs
   - Adjust doses of renally cleared medications
   - Provide patient education on medication safety
   - Monitor for drug-drug interactions
   - Support initiation and titration of guideline-directed therapies
   - Order labs and recommend therapy adjustments under collaborative practice agreements
   - Identify patients requiring further evaluation or referral
   - Citation: Practical Approach to Detection and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease for the Primary Care Clinician

4. **Social Workers**
   - Address psychosocial barriers to care (depression, anxiety, health literacy challenges)
   - Connect patients to community resources
   - Assist with insurance navigation and medication access
   - Support advance care planning for patients with advanced CKD
   - Citation: Chronic Kidney Disease Diagnosis and Management: A Review
