# Vaccination Adherence Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 5
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/af24abec-68c4-4566-a6a5-81126a670850

### Patient-Focused Interventions

1. **Patient Reminder and Recall Systems**
   - Deliver via letters, text messages, phone calls, postcards, or EHR messages
   - Improve vaccination rates by 8-9% (risk ratio 1.28, 95% CI 1.23-1.35)
   - Effective across all modalities (postcards, texts, autodialer, phone calls, letters)
   - Citation: Addressing Common Barriers in Adult Immunizations: A Review of Interventions

2. **Educational Interventions**
   - Provide tailored, culturally competent education in plain language
   - Particularly effective for populations with low health literacy or limited access
   - Citation: A Review of the Key Factors to Improve Adult Immunization Coverage Rates

3. **Financial Incentives**
   - Reduce out-of-pocket costs
   - Provide financial incentives, especially for low socioeconomic status populations
   - Directly address cost barriers to improve uptake
   - Citation: Adult Vaccination Coverage in the United States: A Database Analysis and Literature Review Improvement Strategies

### Provider and System-Level Interventions

1. **Provider Reminders**
   - Implement EHR prompts to identify eligible patients
   - Review vaccination status at every visit
   - Citation: A Review of the Key Factors to Improve Adult Immunization Coverage Rates

2. **Standing Orders**
   - Authorize nurses, pharmacists, and other healthcare professionals to assess eligibility and administer vaccines
   - Eliminate need for physician examination or individual orders
   - Citation: Addressing Common Barriers in Adult Immunizations: A Review of Interventions

3. **Audit and Feedback**
   - Track provider performance in delivering vaccines
   - Provide regular feedback on vaccination rates compared to goals or peers
   - Citation: Effective Interventions to Increase Routine Childhood Immunization Coverage

4. **Immunization Information Systems**
   - Use centralized registries to identify and prompt vaccination for eligible patients
   - Ensure bidirectional communication with EHRs
   - Citation: Adult Vaccination Coverage in the United States

5. **Strong Provider Recommendation**
   - Deliver clear, strong recommendations for vaccination (odds ratio 3.4, 95% CI 2.5-4.6)
   - Ensure consistent messaging across care teams
   - Identify vaccine champions within practices
   - Citation: A Review of the Key Factors to Improve Adult Immunization Coverage Rates

6. **Expanded Access**
   - Offer extended clinic hours
   - Establish vaccine-only clinics
   - Provide on-site vaccination in hospitals, emergency departments, or community settings
   - Citation: Addressing Common Barriers in Adult Immunizations

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators**
   - Systematically review immunization status at every encounter
   - Use EHR prompts to identify patients due for vaccines
   - Coordinate reminder/recall systems (phone, text, mail)
   - Facilitate scheduling and address barriers like transportation or cost
   - Ensure documentation in immunization registries
   - Citation: A Review of the Key Factors to Improve Adult Immunization Coverage Rates

2. **Community Health Workers (CHWs)**
   - Conduct home visits to reach underserved populations
   - Track and link defaulters to vaccination services
   - Provide culturally tailored education
   - Update contact information for hard-to-reach populations
   - Verify immunization records to support data quality
   - Citation: Effective Interventions to Increase Routine Childhood Immunization Coverage

3. **Pharmacists**
   - Screen for vaccine eligibility during all patient encounters
   - Provide strong recommendations for needed vaccines
   - Administer vaccines under standing orders
   - Use motivational interviewing to address hesitancy
   - Ensure bidirectional communication with immunization registries
   - Collaborate with other providers to reduce missed opportunities
   - Citation: Addressing Common Barriers in Adult Immunizations

4. **Pharmacy Technicians**
   - Identify eligible patients during prescription processing
   - Prepare documentation for vaccination
   - Assist with patient education about vaccines
   - Administer vaccines where permitted after accredited training
   - Support workflow to expand vaccination capacity
   - Citation: Adult Vaccination Coverage in the United States

5. **Nurse Care Managers**
   - Assess immunization status during routine care
   - Deliver patient education about vaccines
   - Provide reminders for upcoming or overdue vaccines
   - Administer vaccines during routine care visits
   - Coordinate with primary care and public health teams
   - Ensure follow-up for patients who miss appointments
   - Citation: A Review of the Key Factors to Improve Adult Immunization Coverage Rates

6. **Social Workers**
   - Identify and address social determinants that impede vaccine access
   - Connect patients to community resources or financial assistance programs
   - Provide tailored education to address concerns
   - Build trust among vulnerable populations
   - Citation: Effective Interventions to Increase Routine Childhood Immunization Coverage

### Multicomponent Strategies

1. **Combined Approaches**
   - Implement patient reminders, education, expanded access, and provider/system changes simultaneously
   - Address multiple barriers to maximize impact
   - Particularly effective in marginalized or high-risk populations
   - Citation: Adult Vaccination Coverage in the United States
