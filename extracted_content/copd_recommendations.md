# COPD Care Management Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 13
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/0ec93615-94ba-43c3-9274-d7c6a7281e3e

### Chronic Care Model Framework

1. **Integrated Care Approach**
   - Implement the Chronic Care Model framework
   - Integrate patient-, provider-, and system-level interventions
   - Support proactive, multidisciplinary, and patient-centered care
   - Include delivery system redesign, decision support, clinical information systems
   - Provide self-management support and community linkages
   - Citation: COPD management research

### Key Interventions

1. **Smoking Cessation**
   - Offer evidence-based smoking cessation support to all patients
   - Prioritize as single most effective intervention for reducing mortality
   - Target slowing disease progression
   - Citation: COPD management research

2. **Vaccinations**
   - Administer annual influenza vaccines
   - Provide pneumococcal vaccines
   - Target reduction of exacerbations and hospitalizations
   - Citation: COPD management research

3. **Pulmonary Rehabilitation**
   - Offer to all symptomatic patients regardless of disease stage
   - Target improvements in symptoms, exercise tolerance, and quality of life
   - Reduce exacerbations and hospitalizations
   - Citation: COPD management research

4. **Self-Management Support and Action Plans**
   - Implement structured education programs
   - Provide written action plans for exacerbations
   - Deliver iterative, personalized behavioral interventions
   - Incorporate behavioral change techniques
   - Establish regular feedback between patients and providers
   - Target health-related quality of life improvement
   - Reduce respiratory-related hospital admissions
   - Citation: COPD management research

5. **Integrated Disease Management (IDM)**
   - Implement multidisciplinary interventions
   - Coordinate across multiple treatment settings
   - Target quality of life improvement
   - Reduce respiratory-related hospital admissions
   - Citation: COPD management research

6. **Case Management and Care Coordination**
   - Establish structured follow-up protocols
   - Coordinate among healthcare providers
   - Target reduced acute care utilization
   - Support adherence to evidence-based interventions
   - Citation: COPD management research

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators**
   - Facilitate transitions between care settings
   - Coordinate multidisciplinary care team
   - Ensure follow-up after exacerbations
   - Monitor adherence to treatment plans
   - Citation: COPD management research

2. **Respiratory Therapists**
   - Deliver pulmonary rehabilitation components
   - Provide inhaler technique education
   - Conduct breathing exercises training
   - Support smoking cessation efforts
   - Citation: COPD management research

3. **Nurse Care Managers**
   - Implement self-management education
   - Monitor symptoms and medication adherence
   - Coordinate care across providers
   - Facilitate early intervention for exacerbations
   - Citation: COPD management research

4. **Pharmacists**
   - Perform medication therapy management
   - Review inhaler technique
   - Identify drug interactions
   - Support smoking cessation
   - Ensure vaccination compliance
   - Citation: COPD management research

5. **Social Workers**
   - Address social determinants affecting COPD management
   - Connect patients to community resources
   - Provide emotional support and counseling
   - Assist with advance care planning
   - Citation: COPD management research

6. **Community Health Workers**
   - Conduct home assessments for triggers
   - Reinforce self-management education
   - Support smoking cessation efforts
   - Facilitate access to community resources
   - Citation: COPD management research
