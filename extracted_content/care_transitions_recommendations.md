# Care Transitions Management Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 10
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/119864f1-f94e-42fd-8535-9e2433c3287d

### Multidisciplinary, Bundled Transitional Care Models

1. **Naylor and Coleman Models**
   - Employ advanced practice providers or transitions coaches
   - Deliver bundled approach including in-hospital engagement, post-discharge home visits, and ongoing telephone support
   - Emphasize patient and caregiver empowerment
   - Provide self-management education
   - Conduct medication reconciliation
   - Target reduction in 30-day hospital readmissions and healthcare costs
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

2. **Patient- and Family-Centered Interventions**
   - Incorporate health assessment
   - Perform medication reconciliation
   - Conduct discharge planning
   - Provide symptom management
   - Offer emotional support
   - Tailor interventions to patient and family needs
   - Target significant reductions in hospital readmissions
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

3. **Low- and Medium-Complexity Transitional Care**
   - Implement low-complexity interventions (structured discharge planning, brief follow-up) for reducing 30- and 180-day readmissions and ED visits
   - Use medium-complexity interventions (adding medication management or more intensive follow-up) for 90-day effectiveness and improving medication adherence
   - Consider high-complexity interventions for improving patient satisfaction
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

4. **Intensive Interdisciplinary Programs for High-Need Patients**
   - Implement the SafeMed model for high-need, high-cost Medicaid patients
   - Emphasize rapid primary care follow-up
   - Provide intensive home visits
   - Establish robust care coordination
   - Target reductions in 30-day readmissions, hospitalizations, ED visits, and overall medical expenditures
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

### Skilled Nursing Facility Transitions

1. **Structured Communication**
   - Establish bi-directional communication between hospital and SNF providers
   - Conduct regular interdisciplinary meetings
   - Eliminate hierarchy in communication
   - Foster shared learning environments
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

2. **Standardized Processes**
   - Use standardized transfer forms
   - Perform comprehensive medication reconciliation
   - Establish clear accountability for information transfer
   - Follow American Diabetes Association recommendations for transitions
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

### Transitions from Incarceration

1. **Comprehensive Discharge Planning**
   - Align with community standards
   - Provide health records
   - Ensure sufficient supply of medications
   - Deliver patient education
   - Establish direct linkage to community-based care
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

2. **Transitions Clinic Network Model**
   - Integrate community health workers with lived experience of incarceration into primary care teams
   - Target reductions in ED utilization and recidivism
   - Address both medical and social determinants of health
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators and Nurse Care Managers**
   - Lead interdisciplinary predischarge planning
   - Ensure postdischarge follow-up
   - Maintain care continuity
   - Establish clear accountability for each transition step
   - Facilitate patient and caregiver engagement
   - Provide tailored education on disease management, warning signs, and self-care strategies
   - Empower patients to participate in decision-making
   - Coordinate rapid primary care follow-up appointments
   - Arrange home visits for high-need or high-risk patients
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

2. **Community Health Workers (CHWs)**
   - Conduct home visits to reinforce discharge instructions
   - Assess home safety
   - Provide self-management education
   - Support medication adherence and symptom monitoring
   - Assist with transportation and appointment scheduling
   - For formerly incarcerated individuals, leverage lived experience to build trust and address unique barriers
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

3. **Pharmacists and Pharmacy Technicians**
   - Perform comprehensive medication reconciliation at all transition points
   - Identify and resolve medication discrepancies
   - Provide medication education and adherence support
   - Ensure appropriate medication access post-discharge
   - Conduct post-discharge medication reviews
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care

4. **Social Workers**
   - Assess social determinants of health affecting care transitions
   - Connect patients to community resources for housing, food, transportation, and financial assistance
   - Facilitate insurance enrollment and medication access programs
   - Provide psychosocial support and counseling
   - For justice-involved individuals, address legal barriers and coordinate with probation/parole services
   - Citation: Carceral Health Care
