# Preventive Screening Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 12
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/4f8166f6-904a-47bd-8ea6-70181aa9730c

### Patient-Focused Interventions

1. **Patient Navigation**
   - Assist individuals in overcoming logistical, linguistic, and cultural barriers
   - Provide education on screening importance and procedures
   - Facilitate scheduling and follow-up
   - Tailor navigation to address specific patient barriers (language, transportation)
   - Integrate with population health IT systems
   - Target high-risk and vulnerable populations
   - Citation: American Cancer Society and American College of Gastroenterology recommendations

2. **Personalized Outreach**
   - Implement direct mail of FIT/FOBT kits for colorectal cancer screening
   - Include low-literacy instructions and postage-paid return envelopes
   - Use telephone reminders (personal or automated) rather than mailed reminders alone
   - Combine multiple outreach modalities (letters, calls, texts)
   - Citation: Preventive screening research

3. **Patient Education**
   - Deliver via brochures, videos, or risk counseling
   - Combine with other interventions for greater effectiveness
   - Implement shared decision-making models (e.g., Project ACTIVE)
   - Engage patients in prioritizing preventive care goals
   - Develop action plans for screening completion
   - Citation: Project ACTIVE research

4. **Financial Incentives**
   - Consider modest incentives for screening completion
   - Recognize limited effectiveness compared to navigation or direct outreach
   - Citation: Preventive screening research

### Provider and Health System Interventions

1. **Provider Recommendation and Counseling**
   - Encourage direct clinician recommendations
   - Combine with patient education and reminders
   - Train providers on effective communication about screening
   - Citation: Preventive screening research

2. **Organizational Changes**
   - Establish dedicated prevention clinics
   - Assign nonphysician staff (medical assistants or navigators) to manage preventive care
   - Implement standing orders for routine screenings
   - Redesign workflows to prioritize preventive care
   - Citation: Meta-analyses on organizational interventions

3. **Health Information Technology**
   - Implement EHR-based tools and population health IT systems
   - Use provider reminders or audit-and-feedback mechanisms
   - Systematically identify patients overdue for screening
   - Track outreach efforts and monitor progress
   - Utilize practice facilitation and academic detailing
   - Support practices in implementing evidence-based interventions
   - Citation: Preventive screening research

4. **Multifaceted, Multilevel Interventions**
   - Address barriers at patient, provider, and system levels
   - Tailor to populations facing multiple structural barriers
   - Combine mailed outreach, patient navigation, and automated reminders
   - Target sustained improvements in screening adherence
   - Citation: Preventive screening research

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators**
   - Identify patients overdue for screening using registries or EHR dashboards
   - Conduct targeted outreach (calls, letters, texts)
   - Facilitate scheduling
   - Track completion
   - Coordinate follow-up for abnormal results
   - Ensure navigation across care transitions
   - Address logistical barriers (transportation, language)
   - Citation: Preventive screening research

2. **Community Health Workers**
   - Provide culturally and linguistically tailored education
   - Conduct home visits or community outreach
   - Deliver reminders
   - Offer navigation support
   - Help patients overcome barriers (transportation, health literacy, mistrust)
   - Serve as liaisons between patients and healthcare system
   - Citation: Community Preventive Services Task Force recommendations

3. **Pharmacists**
   - Screen for social determinants of health during medication reviews
   - Educate patients about preventive screenings
   - Refer or directly connect patients to screening services
   - Leverage accessibility to identify gaps
   - Provide reminders or motivational interviewing
   - Citation: Preventive screening research

4. **Pharmacy Technicians**
   - Support pharmacists in preventive screening initiatives
   - Help with administrative aspects of screening programs
   - Citation: Preventive screening research

5. **Nurse Care Managers**
   - Conduct risk assessments
   - Provide education on screening importance
   - Develop personalized care plans
   - Monitor adherence to screening recommendations
   - Coordinate care across providers
   - Citation: Preventive screening research

6. **Social Workers**
   - Address social determinants affecting screening adherence
   - Connect patients to resources for transportation, insurance, and financial assistance
   - Provide emotional support and counseling
   - Help navigate complex healthcare systems
   - Citation: Preventive screening research
