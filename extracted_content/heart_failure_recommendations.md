# Heart Failure Care Management Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 1
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/2387c84c-8185-4f12-a15f-1168b1b5b17a

### Role-Specific Recommendations

#### Care Coordinators and Nurse Care Managers

1. **Post-Discharge Follow-Up**
   - Oversee care transitions and ensure timely post-discharge follow-up within 7 days to reduce readmissions and optimize therapy.
   - Develop and communicate individualized care plans across the care team.
   - Citation: Disease Management Interventions for Heart Failure (Cochrane Review)

2. **Symptom Monitoring and GDMT Optimization**
   - Monitor for HF progression, coordinate laboratory and diagnostic testing.
   - Facilitate rapid initiation and titration of guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT).
   - Citation: 2024 ACC Expert Consensus Decision Pathway for Treatment of Heart Failure

3. **Patient Education and Self-Care**
   - Reinforce self-care education, including daily weights, symptom recognition, and action plans for decompensation.
   - Lead patient and caregiver education, focusing on skill-building for medication management, dietary adherence, and early symptom reporting.
   - Citation: Effects of Self-Management Interventions on Heart Failure: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

4. **Psychosocial Assessment**
   - Screen for depression, frailty, health literacy, and social isolation.
   - Address barriers to self-care and adherence.
   - Citation: Addressing Social Determinants of Health in the Care of Patients With Heart Failure

#### Community Health Workers (CHWs)

1. **Home Visits and Cultural Support**
   - Provide culturally and linguistically appropriate peer support.
   - Conduct home visits to assess living conditions and medication adherence.
   - Citation: Implementation Science to Achieve Equity in Heart Failure Care

2. **Social Determinants of Health Intervention**
   - Address social determinants of health by assisting with insurance reinstatement, transportation, and employment resources.
   - Foster trust through shared community background.
   - Citation: Addressing Social Determinants of Health in the Care of Patients With Heart Failure

3. **Self-Management Support**
   - Support self-management and medication adherence through coaching.
   - Provide appointment reminders and facilitate access to healthy behaviors and local services.
   - Citation: Effects of Self-Management Interventions on Heart Failure

#### Pharmacists

1. **Medication Reconciliation**
   - Conduct comprehensive medication reconciliation at transitions of care.
   - Identify and resolve drug-related problems.
   - Citation: Multidisciplinary Strategies for the Management of Heart Failure Patients at High Risk for Admission

2. **GDMT Optimization**
   - Optimize GDMT by monitoring for drug interactions.
   - Titrate medications in collaboration with the care team.
   - Citation: 2024 ACC Expert Consensus Decision Pathway for Treatment of Heart Failure

3. **Patient Education**
   - Provide patient counseling on medication use, adherence, and potential adverse effects.
   - Educate patients on the rationale, dosing, and side effects of each medication.
   - Citation: New Models for Heart Failure Care Delivery

#### Pharmacy Technicians

1. **Medication Access Coordination**
   - Coordinate medication access and manage refills.
   - Monitor adherence to ensure patients have uninterrupted access to medications.
   - Citation: Optimal Effectiveness of Heart Failure Management

#### Social Workers

1. **Resource Navigation**
   - Assess social needs and connect patients to community resources.
   - Address housing instability, food insecurity, and transportation barriers.
   - Citation: Addressing Social Determinants of Health in the Care of Patients With Heart Failure

2. **Psychosocial Support**
   - Provide counseling for adjustment to chronic illness.
   - Address caregiver burden and facilitate advance care planning discussions.
   - Citation: Multidisciplinary Strategies for the Management of Heart Failure Patients

### General Recommendations for Heart Failure Management

1. **Multidisciplinary Team-Based Care**
   - Implement multidisciplinary team-based care involving nurses, pharmacists, social workers, and cardiologists.
   - Emphasize care coordination, shared decision-making, and patient-centered approaches.
   - Citation: Multidisciplinary Strategies for the Management of Heart Failure Patients at High Risk for Admission

2. **Case Management Programs**
   - Develop nurse-led, protocol-driven case management programs.
   - Include structured follow-up, medication titration, patient education, and symptom monitoring.
   - Citation: Disease Management Interventions for Heart Failure

3. **Self-Management Support**
   - Implement self-management interventions encompassing patient education, behavioral support, and telemonitoring.
   - Focus on symptom recognition, therapy adherence, and early response to decompensation signs.
   - Citation: Effects of Self-Management Interventions on Heart Failure

4. **Address Social Determinants of Health**
   - Tailor interventions to sociodemographic factors, health literacy, and access barriers.
   - Integrate social work services to address logistical barriers.
   - Citation: Addressing Social Determinants of Health in the Care of Patients With Heart Failure

5. **Digital Health Integration**
   - Utilize digital health tools, including telemonitoring, mobile applications, and remote monitoring devices.
   - Implement EHR-based clinical decision support tools to increase GDMT prescription rates.
   - Citation: Implementation Science to Achieve Equity in Heart Failure Care
