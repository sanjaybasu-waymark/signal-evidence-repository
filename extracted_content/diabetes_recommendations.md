# Diabetes Management Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 21
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/9c8cbb68-63a3-4087-855b-cca3ced5f64c

### Role-Specific Recommendations

#### Care Coordinators/Case Managers

1. **Care Coordination and Navigation**
   - Oversee care transitions and facilitate communication among providers
   - Ensure follow-up on abnormal results
   - Track completion of recommended screenings (e.g., A1C, eye exams)
   - Help patients navigate the health system
   - Citation: Improving Care and Promoting Health in Populations: Standards of Care in Diabetes-2025

2. **Barrier Assessment and Intervention**
   - Screen for and address barriers to self-management
   - Address functional, cognitive, financial, and logistical challenges
   - Coordinate referrals to specialists or community resources as needed
   - Citation: Improving Care and Promoting Health in Populations: Standards of Care in Diabetes-2025

#### Community Health Workers (CHWs)

1. **Culturally Tailored Education and Support**
   - Provide culturally tailored diabetes self-management education
   - Support behavior change
   - Conduct outreach
   - Connect patients to community and social services
   - Citation: Role of Community Health Workers in Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus Self-Management: A Scoping Review

2. **Patient Advocacy and Support**
   - Offer instrumental, informational, and emotional support
   - Advocate for patients
   - Assist with basic health education and referrals
   - Provide bi-directional feedback to clinical teams
   - Citation: Roles for Community Health Workers in Diabetes Prevention and Management in Low-/Middle-Income Countries

3. **Clinical Task Support**
   - Perform clinical tasks under direct supervision of licensed health professionals
   - Work within scope defined by state law
   - Citation: Prevention or Delay of Diabetes and Associated Comorbidities: Standards of Care in Diabetes 2025

#### Pharmacists

1. **Medication Management**
   - Conduct comprehensive medication reviews
   - Identify and resolve drug-related problems
   - Provide medication counseling
   - Support adherence
   - Citation: Care Management for Patients With Type 2 Diabetes: The Roles of Nurses, Pharmacists, and Social Workers

2. **Medication Adjustment and Monitoring**
   - Titrate or adjust diabetes medications under protocol
   - Monitor for adverse effects
   - Reinforce self-management education
   - Focus on patients with polypharmacy or complex regimens
   - Citation: Care Management for Patients With Type 2 Diabetes: The Roles of Nurses, Pharmacists, and Social Workers

#### Pharmacy Technicians

1. **Medication Support**
   - Perform medication reconciliation
   - Assist with refill coordination
   - Identify adherence barriers
   - Facilitate communication between patients and pharmacists
   - Help with insurance or access issues
   - Citation: Care Management Processes Important for High-Quality Diabetes Care

#### Nurse Care Managers

1. **Clinical Management and Education**
   - Deliver diabetes self-management education
   - Monitor clinical parameters (e.g., A1C, blood pressure)
   - Provide lifestyle counseling
   - Coordinate care
   - Citation: Care Management for Patients With Type 2 Diabetes: The Roles of Nurses, Pharmacists, and Social Workers

2. **Protocol-Based Medication Management**
   - Adjust medications under protocol
   - Conduct follow-up after visits or hospitalizations
   - Address social determinants of health
   - Citation: Care Management for Patients With Type 2 Diabetes: The Roles of Nurses, Pharmacists, and Social Workers

#### Social Workers

1. **Psychosocial Assessment and Support**
   - Assess and address psychosocial needs
   - Address mental health, housing, food insecurity, and financial barriers
   - Connect patients to community resources
   - Provide counseling
   - Support care planning for complex social or behavioral health needs
   - Citation: Care Management for Patients With Type 2 Diabetes: The Roles of Nurses, Pharmacists, and Social Workers

### General Recommendations for Diabetes Management

1. **Integrated, Person-Centered Approaches**
   - Tailor care to individual comorbidities, preferences, and social context
   - Implement system-level strategies to reduce health disparities
   - Focus on racial and ethnic minorities and those facing socioeconomic barriers
   - Citation: Improving Care and Promoting Health in Populations: Standards of Care in Diabetes-2025

2. **Chronic Care Model Implementation**
   - Implement four or more CCM elements: delivery system design, self-management support, decision support, clinical information systems, community resources, and health systems
   - Operationalize through Patient-Centered Medical Home (PCMH), Accountable Care Organizations (ACOs), and Accountable Health Communities Model
   - Citation: Improving Care and Promoting Health in Populations: Standards of Care in Diabetes-2025

3. **Team-Based and Interprofessional Care**
   - Empower nurses or pharmacists to adjust medications under protocol
   - Tailor interprofessional teams to specific patient subpopulations
   - Focus on reducing inequities in care and outcomes
   - Citation: Care Management for Patients With Type 2 Diabetes: The Roles of Nurses, Pharmacists, and Social Workers

4. **Care Management Processes**
   - Implement systems for identifying and reminding patients due for testing
   - Conduct after-visit follow-up by nonclinicians
   - Use guideline-based clinician reminders for preventive services during clinic visits
   - Citation: Care Management Processes Important for High-Quality Diabetes Care

5. **Social Needs and Health Disparities Interventions**
   - Conduct systematic screening for social determinants of health
   - Connect patients to community resources
   - Provide social support (food delivery, benefits assistance)
   - Citation: Improving Care and Promoting Health in Populations: Standards of Care in Diabetes-2025
