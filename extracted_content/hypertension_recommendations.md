# Hypertension Management Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 20
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/d914c295-05e0-493c-900d-edd1dcd2237b

### Role-Specific Recommendations

#### Care Coordinators

1. **Care Coordination and Follow-up**
   - Coordinate follow-up appointments and monitor care plan adherence
   - Facilitate communication among all team members
   - Track patient progress toward blood pressure goals
   - Address barriers to care such as insurance or transportation issues
   - Ensure patients are linked to appropriate community and social services
   - Citation: Implementation Strategies to Improve Blood Pressure Control in the United States

#### Community Health Workers (CHWs)

1. **Patient Education and Support**
   - Provide culturally sensitive education on hypertension and its management
   - Support self-measured blood pressure (SMBP) monitoring
   - Facilitate lifestyle changes such as dietary modification and increased physical activity
   - Citation: Implementation Strategies to Improve Blood Pressure Control in the United States

2. **Community Connection and Outreach**
   - Connect patients to community resources
   - Bridge gaps in health literacy, language, and trust
   - Conduct outreach and advocacy, especially in resource-constrained or underserved settings
   - Provide bi-directional feedback to clinical teams regarding patient engagement and barriers
   - Citation: 2017 ACC/AHA/AAPA/ABC/ACPM/AGS/APhA/ASH/ASPC/NMA/PCNA Guideline for the Prevention, Detection, Evaluation, and Management of High Blood Pressure in Adults

#### Pharmacists

1. **Medication Management**
   - Conduct medication reconciliation
   - Titrate antihypertensive therapy according to established protocols
   - Counsel patients on medication adherence
   - Identify or manage side effects
   - Optimize pharmacotherapy to improve blood pressure control
   - Citation: 2017 ACC/AHA/AAPA/ABC/ACPM/AGS/APhA/ASH/ASPC/NMA/PCNA Guideline for the Prevention, Detection, Evaluation, and Management of High Blood Pressure in Adults

#### Pharmacy Technicians

1. **Medication Access Support**
   - Synchronize medication refills
   - Provide refill reminders
   - Monitor adherence
   - Support pharmacists and the broader care team
   - Citation: Implementation Strategies to Improve Blood Pressure Control in the United States

#### Nurse Care Managers

1. **Clinical Monitoring and Education**
   - Monitor blood pressure
   - Provide health coaching
   - Implement stepped-care protocols for medication adjustment
   - Support SMBP
   - Educate patients on lifestyle modification
   - Reinforce adherence to treatment plans
   - Coordinate medication adjustments with prescribers as needed
   - Citation: Implementation Strategies to Improve Blood Pressure Control in the United States

#### Social Workers

1. **Social Determinants Assessment and Support**
   - Assess and address social determinants of health (housing instability, food insecurity, financial barriers)
   - Provide resource navigation
   - Support behavioral change
   - Facilitate access to insurance, medication assistance programs, and community-based services
   - Citation: Implementation Strategies to Improve Blood Pressure Control in the United States

### General Recommendations for Hypertension Management

1. **Accurate Blood Pressure Measurement**
   - Standardized, accurate office blood pressure measurement
   - Staff training in proper patient preparation, positioning, cuff selection
   - Use of validated automated devices to obtain multiple readings
   - Citation: American Heart Association and American Medical Association guidelines

2. **Self-Measured Blood Pressure (SMBP) Monitoring**
   - Patients measure BP at home using validated devices
   - Combined with patient education, structured monitoring protocols, and telemonitoring
   - Integration of SMBP data into electronic health records
   - Timely communication between patients and care teams
   - Citation: AMA MAP Framework (Measure accurately, Act rapidly, Partner with patients)

3. **Team-Based Care**
   - Multidisciplinary approach involving primary care providers, nurses, pharmacists, dietitians, social workers, and community health workers
   - Clear delineation of team member roles
   - Systems-level support (EHRs and clinical decision support tools)
   - Organizational change to support care coordination and delegation
   - Citation: American College of Cardiology and American Heart Association guidelines

4. **Quality Improvement Strategies**
   - Stakeholder engagement
   - Clinical staff retraining
   - EHR-based prompts
   - Performance monitoring with feedback
   - Citation: Implementation Strategies to Improve Blood Pressure Control in the United States

5. **Lifestyle Interventions**
   - DASH diet (rich in fruits, vegetables, and low-fat dairy, with reduced saturated fat)
   - Sodium reduction (ideally <2,400 mg/day)
   - Regular aerobic physical activity (3–4 sessions per week, 40 minutes per session)
   - Weight loss
   - Moderation of alcohol intake
   - Tobacco cessation
   - Citation: 2017 ACC/AHA/AAPA/ABC/ACPM/AGS/APhA/ASH/ASPC/NMA/PCNA Guideline for the Prevention, Detection, Evaluation, and Management of High Blood Pressure in Adults
