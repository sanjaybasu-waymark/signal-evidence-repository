# Postnatal Care Management Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 2
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/e91ee752-8e44-4ce9-9f82-d4e9ead52908

### Specific Interventions

#### Structure and Timing of Care

1. **Ongoing, Individualized Care**
   - Initial contact with an obstetric care provider should occur within the first 3 weeks postpartum
   - Comprehensive assessment by 12 weeks
   - Coordinated care teams and clear transitions to primary care
   - Citation: ACOG Committee Opinion No. 736: Optimizing Postpartum Care

2. **Home Visits**
   - Conducted by healthcare professionals or trained lay supporters
   - Can improve maternal satisfaction, exclusive breastfeeding rates, and infant healthcare utilization
   - Individualized home-based care can reduce postpartum depression scores
   - Citation: Schedules for Home Visits in the Early Postpartum Period

3. **Early and Frequent Follow-up**
   - Prompt office or home visits within 48-72 hours of hospital discharge
   - Additional contacts as needed
   - Facilitates early detection of complications and supports maternal-infant bonding
   - Citation: Postpartum Care: An Approach to the Fourth Trimester

4. **Comprehensive Biopsychosocial Assessment**
   - Screening for postpartum depression, intimate partner violence
   - Management of chronic diseases
   - Early identification and intervention for mental health and social risks
   - Citation: Committee Opinion No. 666: Optimizing Postpartum Care

#### Specific Interventions

1. **Breastfeeding Support**
   - Peer support programs and lactation consultants
   - Increases breastfeeding initiation and duration
   - Associated with higher rates of any or exclusive breastfeeding at 1-6 months
   - Citation: Delivery Strategies for Postpartum Care: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

2. **Contraception Counseling**
   - Immediate postpartum contraception counseling and provision
   - Reduces unintended pregnancy and short interpregnancy intervals
   - Offering contraception at the comprehensive postpartum visit, or earlier if desired
   - Citation: Committee Opinion No. 666: Optimizing Postpartum Care

3. **Lifestyle and Cardiovascular Risk Interventions**
   - Particularly important for individuals with adverse pregnancy outcomes (APOs)
   - Referral to structured lifestyle programs (e.g., National Diabetes Prevention Program)
   - Digital health tools and community-based interventions
   - American Heart Association recommends 150 minutes of moderate-intensity exercise per week postpartum
   - Citation: Adverse Pregnancy Outcomes and Cardiovascular Disease Risk: Unique Opportunities for Cardiovascular Disease Prevention in Women

4. **Technology and Reminders**
   - Automated reminders for glucose tolerance testing
   - Improves adherence to recommended postpartum screenings
   - Particularly effective for women with a history of gestational diabetes or preeclampsia
   - Citation: Delivery Strategies for Postpartum Care: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators**
   - Ensure coordinated care teams and clear transitions to primary care
   - Address the long-term health implications of pregnancy complications
   - Citation: ACOG Committee Opinion No. 736: Optimizing Postpartum Care

2. **Community Health Workers**
   - Provide home visits and culturally appropriate support
   - Support breastfeeding initiation and continuation
   - Citation: Schedules for Home Visits in the Early Postpartum Period

3. **Social Workers**
   - Assess social needs and connect patients to community resources
   - Address intimate partner violence and other social risks
   - Citation: Committee Opinion No. 666: Optimizing Postpartum Care

4. **Nurse Care Managers**
   - Conduct comprehensive biopsychosocial assessments
   - Screen for postpartum depression and other mental health concerns
   - Coordinate care for chronic disease management
   - Citation: Postpartum Care: An Approach to the Fourth Trimester
