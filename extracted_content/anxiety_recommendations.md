# Anxiety Disorders Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 18
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/c366ed3b-2b63-4208-ab89-48236397f3d4

### Screening and Assessment

1. **Routine Screening**
   - Implement routine screening for anxiety, especially in high-risk populations
   - Target women, cancer survivors, and children/adolescents
   - Use standardized tools (e.g., GAD-7)
   - Assess symptom severity, comorbidities, functional impairment, and suicide risk
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

2. **Early Identification**
   - Facilitate timely intervention through early identification
   - Target improved outcomes through prompt treatment
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

### Care Models

1. **Stepped-Care Model**
   - Begin with initial assessment, patient education, and lifestyle modifications
   - Escalate to more intensive interventions based on symptom severity and response
   - Implement systematic approach to treatment intensification
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

2. **Collaborative Care Models**
   - Integrate case managers, care coordination, and close collaboration between mental health and primary care
   - Demonstrate improved outcomes compared to usual care
   - Deliver via telephone or internet to enhance scalability and access
   - Target underserved or rural populations
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

### Psychological Interventions

1. **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)**
   - Implement as first-line intervention for anxiety disorders
   - Deliver across individual, group, and digital formats
   - Apply across all age groups
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

2. **Behavioral Activation**
   - Implement as evidence-based therapy for anxiety
   - Target increased engagement in positive activities
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

3. **Mindfulness-Based Interventions**
   - Implement Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR)
   - Offer Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)
   - Provide Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT)
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

4. **Interpersonal Therapy**
   - Implement as evidence-based approach for anxiety
   - Address interpersonal factors contributing to anxiety
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

5. **Structured Psychosocial Interventions**
   - Implement relaxation techniques
   - Provide problem-solving training
   - Tailor to symptom severity, patient preference, and cultural/linguistic needs
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

### Pharmacologic Interventions

1. **First-Line Medications**
   - Prescribe selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs)
   - Consider serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitors (SNRIs)
   - Target symptom reduction and improved functioning
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

2. **Alternative Medications**
   - Consider agomelatine and quetiapine in specific cases
   - Implement pharmacogenomic testing when appropriate
   - Adjust medication selection based on side effect profiles
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

### Monitoring and Quality Improvement

1. **Regular Symptom Monitoring**
   - Use standardized rating scales
   - Track progress systematically
   - Optimize outcomes through measurement-based care
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

2. **Care Manager Role**
   - Assign care managers to track progress
   - Promote engagement in treatment
   - Facilitate stepped adjustments in care
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Primary Care Providers**
   - Implement routine screening for anxiety
   - Initiate first-line treatments
   - Refer to specialists when appropriate
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

2. **Care Coordinators**
   - Track patient progress
   - Facilitate communication between providers
   - Ensure follow-through with referrals
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

3. **Mental Health Specialists**
   - Provide evidence-based psychotherapies
   - Consult on complex cases
   - Support primary care in treatment planning
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research

4. **Nurse Care Managers**
   - Monitor treatment adherence and response
   - Provide patient education
   - Coordinate care across providers
   - Citation: Anxiety disorder research
