# Food Insecurity Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 15
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/13eede4b-114f-43bf-b131-96706e630dc5

### Screening and Identification

1. **Validated Screening Tools**
   - Implement the two-item Hunger Vital Sign (>95% sensitivity, >82% specificity)
   - Use trauma-informed, culturally sensitive approaches
   - Ensure systematic linkage to effective interventions and resources
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

2. **Systematic Screening Process**
   - Integrate food insecurity screening into clinical workflows
   - Document results in electronic health records
   - Establish clear referral pathways for positive screens
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

### Care Management Interventions

1. **Medically Tailored Meals**
   - Provide meals designed for specific nutritional needs of patients with chronic diseases
   - Target improvements in food security, diet quality, glycemic control, blood pressure, and body weight
   - Support self-management of disease
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

2. **Food Prescription Programs**
   - Implement clinician prescriptions for fruits, vegetables, or healthy foods
   - Make prescriptions redeemable at participating retailers or farmers markets
   - Combine with nutrition education for enhanced effectiveness
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

3. **Produce Vouchers**
   - Distribute vouchers for fresh produce
   - Target improvements in dietary intake and food security
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

4. **Clinic-Based Food Pantries**
   - Establish onsite food pantries in healthcare settings
   - Provide immediate relief for identified food-insecure patients
   - Improve access to nutritious foods
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

5. **Food Boxes**
   - Distribute food boxes to food-insecure patients
   - Ensure nutritional adequacy of contents
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

6. **Care Coordination and Navigation**
   - Integrate case managers or social workers
   - Assist patients in accessing federal nutrition programs (SNAP, WIC, TEFAP)
   - Connect to local food resources and community-based organizations
   - Provide assistance with benefit applications
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

7. **Patient Education and Skill Building**
   - Deliver nutrition education
   - Offer cooking classes
   - Provide financial counseling
   - Target long-term improvements in food security and dietary quality
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

### Population Health and Policy-Level Interventions

1. **Federal Nutrition Program Enhancement**
   - Advocate for increased benefit levels
   - Support expanded eligibility
   - Incentivize purchase of healthy foods
   - Citation: American Heart Association recommendations

2. **Community-Level Interventions**
   - Implement unconditional and conditional cash transfers
   - Provide food vouchers and subsidies
   - Target improvements in food security and dietary diversity
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

3. **Policy Advocacy**
   - Support increasing minimum wage
   - Advocate for expanded affordable housing
   - Address structural inequities
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators**
   - Screen for food insecurity using validated tools
   - Connect patients to appropriate food resources
   - Follow up to ensure successful linkage to services
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

2. **Social Workers**
   - Assess broader social determinants affecting food security
   - Assist with applications for federal nutrition programs
   - Coordinate with community food resources
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

3. **Community Health Workers**
   - Conduct home-based assessments of food needs
   - Provide culturally appropriate education on nutrition resources
   - Facilitate community connections to food support systems
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

4. **Dietitians**
   - Develop tailored nutrition plans considering food access limitations
   - Provide practical guidance for healthy eating on limited budgets
   - Support medically tailored meal programs
   - Citation: Food insecurity research

5. **Primary Care Providers**
   - Incorporate food insecurity screening into routine care
   - Write "prescriptions" for food resources
   - Refer to appropriate support services
   - Citation: Food insecurity research
