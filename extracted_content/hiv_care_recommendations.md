# HIV Care Management Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 6
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/199391d4-c535-4ebf-afd0-bf776c2e038e

### Multidisciplinary and Integrated Care Models

1. **Multidisciplinary Team Approach**
   - Implement teams comprising physicians, pharmacists, nurses, social workers, mental health professionals, and patient navigators
   - Coordinate primary, specialty, and psychosocial services through an integrated "medical home" model
   - Reduce fragmentation and improve outcomes through comprehensive care coordination
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Providers of Care for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus: 2024 Update by the HIV Medicine Association of the Infectious Diseases Society of America

2. **Comprehensive Case Management**
   - Implement Medicaid Target Case Management and Health Home programs
   - Focus on increasing CD4 counts and care engagement, particularly for patients with complex needs
   - Provide structured follow-up and care coordination across services
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus: 2020 Update by HIV Medicine Association of the Infectious Diseases Society of America

### Evidence-Based Interventions

1. **Patient Navigation**
   - Assign dedicated navigators to guide patients through the healthcare system
   - Help patients overcome barriers to accessing and maintaining care
   - Provide personalized support for appointment scheduling and follow-up
   - Citation: Health Disparities in HIV Care and Strategies for Improving Equitable Access to Care

2. **Appointment Reminders**
   - Implement systematic reminder systems via multiple channels (text, phone, mail)
   - Reduce missed appointments and improve retention in care
   - Tailor reminder frequency and modality to patient preferences
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Providers of Care for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus

3. **Psychosocial Support**
   - Provide mental health services, support groups, and counseling
   - Address psychological barriers to care engagement and medication adherence
   - Integrate behavioral health into primary care settings
   - Citation: Health Disparities in HIV Care and Strategies for Improving Equitable Access to Care

4. **Transportation Assistance**
   - Offer transportation vouchers, rideshare services, or direct transportation
   - Remove logistical barriers to appointment attendance
   - Coordinate with community services for sustainable solutions
   - Citation: Health Disparities in HIV Care and Strategies for Improving Equitable Access to Care

5. **Data-to-Care Strategies**
   - Use health information systems to identify patients who have fallen out of care
   - Implement targeted outreach to re-engage patients
   - Monitor population-level metrics to identify gaps in care
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus

6. **Differentiated Service Delivery Models**
   - Tailor location, intensity, frequency, and provider of services to individual patient needs
   - Adapt care approaches based on patient stability, geography, and preferences
   - Implement flexible appointment scheduling and service delivery options
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Providers of Care for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus

7. **Rapid ART Initiation**
   - Begin antiretroviral therapy on the day of diagnosis or as soon as feasible
   - Reduce time to viral suppression and improve long-term retention
   - Follow protocols endorsed by the Infectious Diseases Society of America
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus

### Social Determinants and Health Equity

1. **Housing Assistance**
   - Connect patients to stable housing resources and programs
   - Address housing instability as a critical factor in care engagement
   - Coordinate with community housing services and programs
   - Citation: Health Disparities in HIV Care and Strategies for Improving Equitable Access to Care

2. **Food Assistance**
   - Provide access to nutritional support and food resources
   - Screen for food insecurity at regular intervals
   - Connect patients to community food programs and benefits
   - Citation: Health Disparities in HIV Care and Strategies for Improving Equitable Access to Care

3. **Stigma Reduction**
   - Implement staff training on cultural competence and stigma reduction
   - Create welcoming clinical environments for marginalized populations
   - Engage people with HIV in program design and implementation
   - Citation: Health Disparities in HIV Care and Strategies for Improving Equitable Access to Care

4. **Cultural Competence**
   - Provide culturally appropriate care tailored to diverse populations
   - Assess social and economic barriers on an ongoing basis
   - Involve people with HIV meaningfully in program design and implementation
   - Citation: Health Disparities in HIV Care and Strategies for Improving Equitable Access to Care

### Innovative Care Delivery

1. **Telehealth Integration**
   - Implement virtual visits and remote monitoring
   - Expand access and continuity of care for populations facing geographic or structural barriers
   - Address digital literacy and technology access challenges
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Providers of Care for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators**
   - Facilitate communication between multidisciplinary team members
   - Track patient progress through care continuum
   - Identify and address barriers to care engagement
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus

2. **Community Health Workers**
   - Conduct outreach to patients who have fallen out of care
   - Provide culturally appropriate education and support
   - Connect patients to community resources
   - Citation: Health Disparities in HIV Care and Strategies for Improving Equitable Access to Care

3. **Social Workers**
   - Assess and address social determinants of health
   - Connect patients to housing, food, and transportation resources
   - Provide counseling and support for psychosocial issues
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Providers of Care for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus

4. **Pharmacists**
   - Conduct medication reconciliation and adherence counseling
   - Monitor for drug interactions and side effects
   - Implement medication management programs
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus

5. **Nurse Care Managers**
   - Coordinate care across multiple providers and settings
   - Provide patient education on disease management
   - Monitor treatment adherence and clinical outcomes
   - Citation: Primary Care Guidance for Providers of Care for Persons With Human Immunodeficiency Virus
