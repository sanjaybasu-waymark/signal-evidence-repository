# Housing Insecurity Recommendations

## Extracted from OpenEvidence Link 16
URL: https://www.openevidence.com/ask/a3ef0bf2-0fc0-4935-933e-54eff33cc198

### Care Approach

1. **Trauma-Informed Care**
   - Implement trauma-informed approaches to care delivery
   - Recognize high prevalence of trauma exposure in housing-insecure populations
   - Train staff in trauma-informed practices
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

2. **Multidisciplinary Care**
   - Integrate primary care, mental health, social work, and case management
   - Co-locate services to facilitate engagement
   - Develop tailored care plans for complex needs
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

3. **Integrated Care Models**
   - Incorporate legal and financial services
   - Include community health workers or housing advocates
   - Target improved chronic disease outcomes and access to care
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

### Case Management Models

1. **Standard Case Management**
   - Facilitate healthcare navigation
   - Recognize limitations for long-term outcomes
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

2. **Intensive Case Management**
   - Provide higher-intensity support
   - Target individuals with complex needs
   - Improve housing stability and reduce substance use
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

3. **Assertive Community Treatment (ACT)**
   - Implement for individuals with serious mental illness
   - Target improved housing stability
   - Reduce rehospitalizations
   - Demonstrate cost-effectiveness
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

4. **Critical Time Intervention (CTI)**
   - Apply during transitions (hospital discharge, housing placement)
   - Target individuals with high service needs
   - Improve housing stability
   - Reduce rehospitalizations
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

### Housing Interventions

1. **Permanent Supportive Housing**
   - Provide immediate, non-contingent housing
   - Offer optional supportive services
   - Target individuals with high support needs
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

2. **Housing First Approach**
   - Implement non-contingent housing without sobriety requirements
   - Target increased long-term housing stability
   - Reduce emergency department use
   - Improve chronic disease management
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

### Screening and Referral

1. **Standardized Screening**
   - Implement standardized screening tools in healthcare settings
   - Integrate into electronic health records
   - Establish clear response protocols
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

2. **Active Assistance**
   - Provide active help connecting patients to resources
   - Avoid simple paper or electronic referrals
   - Follow up to ensure successful connections
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

### System-Level Interventions

1. **Healthcare System Investment**
   - Invest in affordable housing
   - Develop community partnerships
   - Advocate for policy changes
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

2. **"Give, Partner, Invest" Model**
   - Leverage financial, policy, and workforce levers
   - Engage in multisector collaboration
   - Target broader public health impact
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

### Role-Specific Recommendations

1. **Care Coordinators/Nurse Care Managers**
   - Screen for social determinants of health using structured tools
   - Coordinate referrals to community-based organizations
   - Provide regular follow-up and facilitate warm handoffs
   - Monitor appointment adherence
   - Support care plan implementation
   - Engage in psychosocial support and motivational interviewing
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

2. **Community Health Workers**
   - Serve as bridge between patients and health/social services
   - Provide psychosocial support, health education, and advocacy
   - Assist with navigation of healthcare and social service systems
   - Accompany patients to appointments
   - Help with paperwork
   - Identify and address barriers to medication adherence
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

3. **Social Workers**
   - Conduct comprehensive social needs assessments
   - Connect patients to housing resources and benefits
   - Provide case management and care coordination
   - Advocate for patients within healthcare and social service systems
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research

4. **Pharmacists**
   - Implement medication management strategies for transient patients
   - Provide extended medication supplies
   - Coordinate with shelter-based medication storage programs
   - Citation: Housing insecurity research
