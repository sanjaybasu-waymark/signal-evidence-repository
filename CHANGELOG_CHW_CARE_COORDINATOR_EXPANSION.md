This expansion includes clear delineation of medication reconciliation responsibilities across CHW, Care Coordinator, and Pharmacist roles.

## Community Health Worker (CHW) Protocol Expansion

### New Protocols Added: 15 (PROT076-PROT090)

Based on **C3 Council CHW Core Roles and Competencies** and recent evidence on CHW effectiveness in care management.

#### Core CHW Roles Covered

1. **Cultural Mediation and Health System Navigation** (PROT076)
   - Cultural mediation between patients and healthcare systems
   - Health system navigation and interpretation
   - Addressing cultural beliefs affecting health behaviors

2. **Community Outreach and Engagement** (PROT077)
   - Door-to-door outreach and community events
   - Connecting hard-to-reach populations to services
   - Health insurance enrollment assistance

3. **Medication Adherence Support** (PROT078) ⚠️ **KEY DISTINCTION**
   - **CHWs support medication ADHERENCE, NOT medication reconciliation**
   - Identifying and addressing adherence barriers
   - Patient education and coaching
   - Referral to pharmacists for clinical medication issues

4. **Chronic Disease Self-Management Support** (PROT079-PROT085, PROT089-PROT090)
   - Diabetes self-management (PROT079)
   - Hypertension management (PROT080)
   - Asthma management (PROT081)
   - Chronic disease self-management programs (PROT083)
   - Chronic pain self-management (PROT089)
   - Cardiovascular disease prevention (PROT090)

5. **Maternal and Child Health Support** (PROT082)
   - Prenatal care engagement
   - Breastfeeding support
   - Postpartum depression screening
   - Well-child visit promotion

6. **Care Transitions Support** (PROT084)
   - Post-discharge home visits
   - Medication understanding (referring clinical issues to pharmacist)
   - Follow-up appointment coordination

7. **Health Behavior Change Coaching** (PROT085)
   - Motivational interviewing
   - Lifestyle modification support
   - Goal-setting and action planning

8. **Cancer Screening Navigation** (PROT086)
   - Cancer screening education
   - Addressing barriers to screening
   - Navigation through screening process

9. **Social Needs Screening and Resource Connection** (PROT087)
   - Comprehensive social needs screening
   - Resource identification and connection
   - Benefits application assistance

10. **Mental Health Support and Navigation** (PROT088)
    - Mental health screening
    - Reducing stigma
    - Navigation to mental health services

### CHW Evidence Base

All CHW protocols are grounded in peer-reviewed research demonstrating effectiveness in:
- Improving health outcomes in underserved populations
- Enhancing medication adherence (not reconciliation)
- Increasing healthcare access and utilization
- Reducing health disparities
- Addressing social determinants of health
- Supporting chronic disease self-management

### Key CHW Training Components

Based on Segal et al. (2025) CHW medication adherence training program:
- Knowledge scores improved from 28% to 81% post-training
- Focus on non-clinical support and patient navigation
- Collaboration with pharmacists and healthcare team
- Addressing social and behavioral barriers to health

## Medication Reconciliation: Clear Role Boundaries

### Critical Distinctions

This expansion clarifies medication-related responsibilities across care team roles:

#### **PHARMACISTS** (Clinical Pharmacists)
- **PRIMARY ROLE:** Medication reconciliation
- **Activities:**
  - Obtaining complete medication history from multiple sources
  - Comparing medication lists across care transitions
  - Identifying discrepancies, duplications, omissions
  - Resolving drug interactions and dosing issues
  - Clinical medication review and optimization
  - Medication therapy management
- **Scope:** Clinical medication management requiring pharmacological expertise

#### **PHARMACY TECHNICIANS**
- **SUPPORTIVE ROLE:** Medication reconciliation support under pharmacist supervision
- **Activities:**
  - Obtaining medication histories with training
  - Identifying discrepancies (under pharmacist review)
  - Data collection and documentation
- **Scope:** Technical support, not independent clinical decision-making

#### **CARE COORDINATORS**
- **COORDINATION ROLE:** Medication management coordination
- **Activities:**
  - **Ensuring medication reconciliation occurs** (not performing clinical review)
  - Coordinating pharmacist involvement at transitions
  - Tracking medication changes across providers
  - Facilitating medication-related communication
  - Addressing medication access barriers
  - Monitoring for adherence issues and referring appropriately
- **Scope:** Administrative coordination, not clinical medication review

#### **COMMUNITY HEALTH WORKERS (CHWs)**
- **SUPPORT ROLE:** Medication adherence support
- **Activities:**
  - Supporting patients in taking medications as prescribed
  - Identifying adherence barriers (cost, transportation, health literacy)
  - Patient education about medication importance
  - Motivational interviewing for behavior change
  - Referring complex medication issues to pharmacists
  - Follow-up support after pharmacist interventions
- **Scope:** Non-clinical support, patient navigation, adherence coaching

### Key Terminology

- **Medication RECONCILIATION** (Clinical - Pharmacist): Comparing medication lists, identifying discrepancies, clinical review
- **Medication ADHERENCE Support** (Non-Clinical - CHW): Helping patients take medications correctly, addressing barriers
- **Medication Management COORDINATION** (Administrative - Care Coordinator): Ensuring reconciliation occurs, coordinating services

### Collaborative Model

The most effective approach involves:
1. **Pharmacists** perform medication reconciliation and clinical management
2. **Care Coordinators** ensure reconciliation occurs and coordinate follow-up
3. **CHWs** provide adherence support and identify barriers
4. **All roles** communicate and refer appropriately within scope of practice

## Research Foundation

### Key Sources

1. **C3 Council (CHW Core Consensus Project)**
   - National consensus on CHW roles and competencies
   - 10 core roles and 11 core skills
   - "Connection to community served" as most critical CHW quality

2. **AAACN Care Coordination and Transition Management (CCTM) Standards**
   - 16 standards for RN care coordination practice
   - Applicable across ambulatory, acute, post-acute, and community settings

3. **Wisconsin Care Coordinator Competencies (2021)**
   - Developed from input of nearly 300 care coordination professionals
   - 9 competency domains aligned with wraparound principles

4. **Pharmacists and Medication Reconciliation (Patel et al., 2019)**
   - WHO and ASHP designation of pharmacists for medication reconciliation
   - Evidence of 80% error reduction with pharmacist involvement

5. **CHW Medication Adherence Training (Segal et al., 2025)**
   - 109 CHWs trained in medication adherence support
   - Knowledge improvement from 28% to 81%
   - Focus on non-clinical adherence support

### Evidence Quality

- **Average Quality Score:** 8.4/10 (range: 7.8-9.2)
- **Study Designs:** Predominantly RCTs and systematic reviews
- **Sample Sizes:** Range from 109 to 18,309 participants
- **Publication Years:** 1995-2025 (emphasis on recent evidence 2015-2025)
