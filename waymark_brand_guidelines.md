# Waymark Brand Guidelines for Protocol Library

## Brand Identity Overview

Waymark's brand identity emphasizes creating pathways that connect patients to care and providers to patients, guided by community voices and experiences. The brand communicates warmth, trust, accessibility, and human connection.

## Core Brand Elements

### Primary Colors
- **Lavender**: Primary brand color communicating warmth, trust, and human quality
- **Supporting Colors**: Multi-color palette visible in navigation (blue, orange, purple, teal, pink, dark purple)

### Logo and Typography
- **Logo**: Signature "W" shape that embodies connection between patients and care
- **Typography**: Clean, modern sans-serif fonts for accessibility and readability

### Visual Principles
- **Photography Style**: Authentic and diverse human experiences
- **Illustration Style**: Embraces real community stories and experiences
- **Design Philosophy**: Accessible, equitable pathways to better health

## Brand Messaging

### Core Mission
"Creating more equitable and accessible pathways to better health"

### Key Values
- Community-guided care
- Warmth and trust
- Human connection
- Accessibility and equity
- Authentic experiences

### Target Audience
- Medicaid-enrolled patients and families
- Community health workers and care teams
- Healthcare providers and health plans
- Community partners and organizations

## Design Application for Protocol Library

### Color Palette Implementation
- **Primary**: Lavender (#8B7EC8 or similar) for headers and key elements
- **Secondary**: Soft supporting colors for categorization and navigation
- **Neutral**: Clean whites and light grays for readability
- **Accent**: Warm colors for calls-to-action and highlights

### Typography Hierarchy
- **Headers**: Bold, accessible fonts reflecting Waymark's clean aesthetic
- **Body Text**: High-contrast, readable fonts optimized for healthcare content
- **Navigation**: Clear, intuitive labeling consistent with Waymark's approach

### Layout Principles
- **Pathway-Focused**: Design that guides users through content like healthcare navigation
- **Community-Centered**: Emphasize collaborative and team-based approaches
- **Accessible**: High contrast, clear hierarchy, mobile-optimized design
- **Trustworthy**: Professional yet warm aesthetic building confidence

### Visual Elements
- **Waymark Path**: Incorporate the signature "W" pathway concept in navigation
- **Human-Centered**: Include diverse, authentic imagery when appropriate
- **Connection**: Visual elements that emphasize linking patients to care
- **Equity**: Design choices that reflect accessibility and inclusion

## Implementation Notes

The protocol library should feel like a natural extension of Waymark's main website, maintaining brand consistency while serving the specific needs of healthcare teams implementing population health protocols. The design should communicate both clinical professionalism and the warmth/accessibility that defines Waymark's approach to community-based care.
