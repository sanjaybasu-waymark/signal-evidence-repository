# Camden Coalition Research for Protocol Expansion

## Complex Care Startup Toolkit Components

The Camden Coalition's Complex Care Startup Toolkit provides a comprehensive framework for developing population health protocols across six key components:

### 1. Program Design
Tools to support the understanding and identification of a complex care population, plan a care model and workflow, and anticipate legal and business needs.

**Protocol Applications:**
- Population identification and risk stratification protocols
- Care model design and workflow development
- Legal and regulatory compliance protocols
- Business case development for complex care programs

### 2. Program Operations
Tools to support the implementation of a complex care model including triage, outreach, engagement, intake, and clinical support.

**Protocol Applications:**
- Patient triage and prioritization protocols
- Outreach and engagement strategies
- Intake assessment and care planning protocols
- Clinical support and care coordination protocols

### 3. Data & Process Improvement
Tools to support metric identification, data collection and analysis, and process improvement.

**Protocol Applications:**
- Quality metrics and outcome measurement protocols
- Data collection and analysis protocols
- Continuous quality improvement protocols
- Performance monitoring and evaluation protocols

### 4. Team & Leadership Development
Tools to support the recruitment, training, and management of a complex care team.

**Protocol Applications:**
- Staff recruitment and hiring protocols
- Training and competency development protocols
- Team management and supervision protocols
- Professional development and retention protocols

### 5. Community Mapping & Collaboration
Tools to support multi-sector and community coordination.

**Protocol Applications:**
- Community resource mapping protocols
- Multi-sector partnership development protocols
- Care coordination across organizations protocols
- Community engagement and collaboration protocols

### 6. Communication & Growth of Success
Tools to support broad communication and program scaling.

**Protocol Applications:**
- Stakeholder communication protocols
- Program evaluation and reporting protocols
- Scaling and replication protocols
- Advocacy and policy engagement protocols
