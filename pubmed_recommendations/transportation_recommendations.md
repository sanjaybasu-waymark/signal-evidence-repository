# Transportation Insecurity Recommendations

## Screening and Identification

1. **Implement standardized screening tools**:
   - Use the Accountable Health Communities (AHC) 10-item tool for brief screening
   - Consider the Transportation Security Index (16-item) for more detailed assessment
   - Adopt a two-step approach: brief universal screener followed by detailed assessment if positive

2. **Integrate screening into clinical workflows**:
   - Incorporate transportation screening into electronic health records
   - Conduct screening at intake, transitions of care, and chronic disease management visits
   - Document transportation needs in care plans

3. **Train staff on transportation screening**:
   - Educate providers on the impact of transportation insecurity on health outcomes
   - Train care team members to conduct sensitive, effective screening
   - Ensure proper documentation and referral processes

## Direct Transportation Support

1. **Provide non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT)**:
   - Establish NEMT programs for medical appointments
   - Ensure services accommodate mobility challenges and special needs
   - Create clear eligibility criteria and simple request processes

2. **Implement ride-share partnerships**:
   - Develop partnerships with companies like Uber or Lyft
   - Create voucher programs for medical appointments
   - Establish protocols for requesting and authorizing rides

3. **Distribute transportation resources**:
   - Provide bus passes or transit vouchers
   - Offer taxi vouchers for patients with urgent needs
   - Create shuttle services for high-need areas or populations

4. **Tailor transportation solutions**:
   - Adapt services for older adults and those with mobility challenges
   - Consider cultural and linguistic factors in transportation planning
   - Address specific needs of rural vs. urban populations

## Integration with Care Management

1. **Assign transportation coordination responsibilities**:
   - Designate care coordinators or community health workers to manage transportation needs
   - Include transportation planning in care management protocols
   - Establish follow-up procedures to confirm service utilization

2. **Implement multi-component interventions**:
   - Combine transportation support with patient navigation
   - Integrate with care coordination and counseling
   - Address multiple social determinants simultaneously

3. **Develop care team protocols**:
   - Create clear workflows for addressing transportation barriers
   - Establish communication channels between clinical and transportation services
   - Define roles for nurses, social workers, and community health workers

## Technology-Enabled Solutions

1. **Utilize mobile applications**:
   - Implement ride-booking apps for patients
   - Develop transportation tracking systems
   - Create digital platforms for transportation request management

2. **Expand telehealth services**:
   - Reduce the need for in-person visits through virtual care
   - Ensure telehealth platforms are accessible to patients with limited technology
   - Develop hybrid models of care delivery

3. **Establish community-based care sites**:
   - Bring care closer to patients through federally qualified health centers
   - Create mobile health units for underserved areas
   - Develop satellite clinics in high-need communities

## Implementation and Sustainability

1. **Develop sustainable funding models**:
   - Explore philanthropy, grants, and internal health system funding
   - Advocate for insurance reimbursement for transportation services
   - Create business cases demonstrating return on investment

2. **Address logistical barriers**:
   - Streamline eligibility requirements for transportation programs
   - Reduce administrative complexity
   - Ensure reliable, flexible transportation options

3. **Build community partnerships**:
   - Collaborate with local transportation authorities
   - Partner with community organizations
   - Engage faith-based organizations in transportation solutions

4. **Measure outcomes and impact**:
   - Track appointment attendance rates
   - Monitor emergency department utilization and hospitalizations
   - Assess patient satisfaction and empowerment
   - Evaluate cost-effectiveness of transportation interventions

## Role-Specific Recommendations

### Care Coordinators and Nurse Care Managers

1. **Conduct systematic screening** using validated tools at intake, transitions of care, or during chronic disease management visits
2. **Integrate transportation needs into care plans**
3. **Arrange transportation services** such as NEMT, ride-share vouchers, or bus passes
4. **Follow up to confirm service utilization** and reassess needs at subsequent encounters

### Community Health Workers (CHWs)

1. **Provide culturally appropriate transportation assessment**
2. **Connect patients to community transportation resources**
3. **Accompany patients to appointments** when necessary
4. **Educate patients on available transportation options**
5. **Advocate for improved transportation services** in underserved communities

### Social Workers

1. **Conduct comprehensive social needs assessments** including transportation
2. **Identify funding sources** for transportation assistance
3. **Coordinate with community agencies** providing transportation services
4. **Address multiple social determinants** that may impact transportation access
5. **Develop long-term transportation plans** for patients with chronic conditions

### Primary Care Providers

1. **Recognize transportation insecurity** as a barrier to care
2. **Consider transportation needs** when developing treatment plans
3. **Refer patients to appropriate support services**
4. **Advocate for transportation solutions** within healthcare systems
5. **Consider telehealth options** when appropriate
