# Heart Failure Care Management Recommendations

## Multidisciplinary Team-Based Care

1. **Implement multidisciplinary team-based care** involving nurses, pharmacists, social workers, and cardiologists to reduce mortality and hospitalizations among heart failure patients.

2. **Establish specialized heart failure care teams** in clinic or community settings to improve adherence to guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT), facilitate early recognition of decompensation, and enhance care transitions.

3. **Adopt care coordination models** that emphasize shared decision-making and patient-centered approaches, adaptable to local resources.

## Care Coordinator and Nurse Care Manager Responsibilities

1. **Ensure timely post-discharge follow-up** within 7 days to reduce readmissions and optimize therapy.

2. **Develop and communicate individualized care plans** for heart failure patients.

3. **Monitor for heart failure progression** and coordinate laboratory and diagnostic testing.

4. **Facilitate rapid initiation and titration** of guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT).

5. **Reinforce self-care education** including daily weights, symptom recognition, and action plans for decompensation.

6. **Screen for depression, frailty, health literacy, and social isolation** to address barriers to self-care and adherence.

7. **Lead patient and caregiver education** focusing on skill-building for medication management, dietary adherence, and early symptom reporting.

## Community Health Worker Interventions

1. **Provide culturally and linguistically appropriate peer support** through home visits and help patients navigate community resources.

2. **Address social determinants of health** by assisting with insurance reinstatement, transportation, and employment resources.

3. **Support self-management and medication adherence** through coaching, appointment reminders, and facilitating access to healthy behaviors and local services.

## Pharmacist and Pharmacy Technician Roles

1. **Conduct comprehensive medication reconciliation** at transitions of care.

2. **Optimize GDMT by identifying and resolving drug-related problems** and provide patient counseling on medication use, adherence, and potential adverse effects.

3. **Monitor for drug interactions and titrate medications** in collaboration with the care team.

4. **Coordinate medication access, manage refills, and monitor adherence** to ensure patients have uninterrupted access to medications.

## Self-Management and Patient Activation

1. **Implement self-management interventions** encompassing patient education, behavioral support, and telemonitoring to improve HF knowledge, quality of life, and reduce HF-related hospitalizations.

2. **Empower patients to recognize symptoms, adhere to therapy, and respond** appropriately to early signs of decompensation.

3. **Utilize digital health tools** including telemonitoring, mobile applications, and remote monitoring devices to support self-management and facilitate early intervention.

## Implementation Strategies

1. **Apply implementation science approaches** such as audit and feedback, electronic health record (EHR) nudges, and algorithmic GDMT optimization to improve process measures and clinical outcomes.

2. **Utilize EHR-based clinical decision support tools** to increase GDMT prescription rates, particularly for beta-blockers.

3. **Address social determinants of health** with interventions tailored to sociodemographic factors, health literacy, and access barriers to improve engagement and outcomes in diverse populations.

4. **Integrate social work into multidisciplinary clinics** to address logistical barriers, showing particular benefit in vulnerable groups.
