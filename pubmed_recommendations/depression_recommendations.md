# Depression Management Recommendations

## Screening and Assessment

1. **Implement routine depression screening**:
   - Use validated tools (e.g., PHQ-9, PHQ-2)
   - Screen high-risk populations (e.g., chronic illness, postpartum women)
   - Assess for suicide risk using standardized measures
   - Document screening results and follow-up plans

2. **Establish systematic assessment protocols**:
   - Create clear workflows for positive screens
   - Assess symptom severity to guide treatment decisions
   - Evaluate impact on daily functioning
   - Screen for comorbid conditions (anxiety, substance use)

## Collaborative Care Models

1. **Implement collaborative care teams**:
   - Integrate care managers (often nurses or social workers)
   - Provide systematic follow-up and measurement-based care
   - Conduct regular PHQ-9 assessments to track progress
   - Coordinate with primary care and psychiatric consultants

2. **Establish structured care management protocols**:
   - Develop registry systems to track patients with depression
   - Implement regular case reviews with psychiatric input
   - Provide patient education and behavioral activation
   - Ensure timely treatment adjustments based on outcomes

3. **Document collaborative care outcomes**:
   - Track depression symptom remission (hazard ratio for remission: 2.48)
   - Monitor time to remission (median 86 days vs. 614 days with usual care)
   - Assess improvements in quality of life and functional status
   - Measure patient satisfaction with care

## Remote and Population Health Approaches

1. **Implement remote monitoring and outreach**:
   - Develop population-based programs using remote monitoring
   - Establish centralized care management systems
   - Create protocols for outreach to patients not improving
   - Utilize digital tools to extend reach of services

2. **Leverage technology for depression management**:
   - Implement digital screening and monitoring tools
   - Provide telehealth options for therapy and medication management
   - Offer mobile applications for self-management support
   - Use automated systems for symptom monitoring

## Evidence-Based Treatments

1. **Provide psychotherapy options**:
   - Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)
   - Behavioral activation
   - Interpersonal therapy
   - Mindfulness-based therapy
   - Problem-solving therapy

2. **Prescribe appropriate pharmacotherapy**:
   - Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs)
   - Serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitors (SNRIs)
   - Individualize medication choice based on side effect profiles
   - Consider comorbidities and patient preferences

3. **Implement combined treatment approaches**:
   - Offer both psychotherapy and pharmacotherapy for severe depression
   - Provide education on benefits of combined treatment
   - Monitor adherence to both treatment modalities
   - Adjust treatment based on response

## Role-Specific Recommendations

### Primary Care Providers

1. **Implement depression care in primary care settings**:
   - Integrate screening into routine visits
   - Provide brief interventions for mild depression
   - Prescribe and monitor antidepressant medications
   - Refer to specialty care when appropriate

2. **Develop referral pathways**:
   - Create clear criteria for specialty mental health referrals
   - Establish relationships with mental health providers
   - Implement warm handoffs to behavioral health
   - Maintain communication during specialty care

### Care Managers/Coordinators

1. **Provide systematic follow-up**:
   - Contact patients regularly between visits
   - Monitor treatment adherence and side effects
   - Track symptom improvement using standardized measures
   - Facilitate communication between providers

2. **Support patient self-management**:
   - Provide education about depression and treatment
   - Help patients develop coping strategies
   - Connect to community resources
   - Assist with treatment adherence

### Mental Health Specialists

1. **Provide consultation to primary care**:
   - Review complex cases
   - Offer treatment recommendations
   - Support medication management decisions
   - Provide guidance on treatment-resistant depression

2. **Deliver specialized interventions**:
   - Provide evidence-based psychotherapy
   - Treat complex or treatment-resistant cases
   - Address comorbid conditions
   - Offer group-based interventions

## Implementation Strategies

1. **Develop integrated care systems**:
   - Co-locate mental health services in primary care
   - Create shared treatment plans
   - Implement regular team communication
   - Use registry tracking systems

2. **Train healthcare providers**:
   - Educate on evidence-based depression treatments
   - Develop skills in brief interventions
   - Address stigma related to mental health
   - Provide ongoing supervision and support

3. **Measure outcomes**:
   - Track symptom improvement with PHQ-9
   - Monitor treatment engagement and adherence
   - Assess functional improvement and quality of life
   - Evaluate patient satisfaction with care
