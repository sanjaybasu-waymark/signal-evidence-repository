# Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Care Management Recommendations

## Key Principles of CKD Care Management

1. **Implement early detection and risk stratification** using eGFR and albuminuria, especially in high-risk groups such as those with diabetes or hypertension.

2. **Establish routine screening protocols** in high-risk populations to enable timely identification and staging of CKD.

3. **Develop individualized follow-up plans** based on CKD stage and risk factors.

4. **Refer patients to nephrology** when they have advanced disease (eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m²), rapidly progressive disease, or refractory hypertension.

## Evidence-Based Interventions

### Blood Pressure Control
1. **Target blood pressure <130/80 mmHg** to slow CKD progression and reduce cardiovascular risk.
2. **Individualize blood pressure targets** based on comorbidities and tolerability.

### Glycemic Management
1. **Maintain hemoglobin A1c ≤7%** for patients with diabetes to reduce the risk or slow progression of CKD.

### Cardiovascular Risk Reduction
1. **Prescribe statin therapy** for most adults with CKD not on dialysis to reduce cardiovascular risk.

### Kidney-Specific Therapies
1. **Utilize ACE inhibitors or ARBs** for patients with albuminuria and hypertension, titrated to maximally tolerated doses.
2. **Monitor for hyperkalemia and changes in serum creatinine** when using renin-angiotensin system blockers.
3. **Prescribe SGLT2 inhibitors** for patients with diabetes and CKD, and increasingly for non-diabetic CKD, to reduce CKD progression and cardiovascular events.
4. **Consider non-steroidal mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists (ns-MRAs)** such as finerenone in select patients with diabetic CKD and albuminuria.
5. **Evaluate GLP-1 receptor agonists** in patients with diabetes and CKD for additional glycemic and cardiovascular benefit.

### Lifestyle Interventions
1. **Recommend a plant-dominant, low-protein, and low-salt diet** to mitigate CKD progression.
2. **Encourage increased physical activity** appropriate to the patient's capabilities.
3. **Support smoking cessation** to improve overall health and slow CKD progression.

### Medication Safety
1. **Avoid nephrotoxins** such as NSAIDs to prevent iatrogenic harm.
2. **Adjust doses of renally cleared medications** based on eGFR.

## Population Health and Team-Based Approaches

### Multidisciplinary Care Teams
1. **Implement patient-centered care teams** including dietitians, pharmacists, educators, and social workers to improve care coordination, medication management, and patient education.

### Structured Education Programs
1. **Provide education for patients and care partners** to promote self-management, informed decision-making, and adherence to therapy.

### Technology-Enabled Care
1. **Utilize telehealth services** to improve access to care, especially for rural or mobility-limited patients.
2. **Implement EHR-based population health management tools** to identify high-risk patients.
3. **Develop and maintain CKD registries** to facilitate early identification of high-risk patients, timely nephrology input, and coordinated care delivery.

## Role-Specific Interventions

### Care Coordinators and Nurse Care Managers
1. **Identify high-risk CKD patients** using EHR-based registries and risk stratification tools.
2. **Develop and update individualized care plans** for CKD patients.
3. **Ensure proactive follow-up** including monitoring of blood pressure, glycemic control, and laboratory parameters.
4. **Facilitate timely nephrology referrals** for patients with advanced or rapidly progressing disease.
5. **Coordinate communication among team members** to ensure comprehensive care.

### Community Health Workers
1. **Build trust with patients** through culturally sensitive approaches.
2. **Deliver culturally tailored education** about CKD management.
3. **Provide care navigation** particularly for underserved and high-risk populations.
4. **Address social determinants of health** that may impact CKD outcomes.
5. **Support medication adherence** through education and practical assistance.
6. **Connect patients to community resources** to address non-medical needs.

### Pharmacists and Pharmacy Technicians
1. **Perform medication reconciliation** to ensure appropriate therapy.
2. **Identify and mitigate nephrotoxic drugs** that could worsen kidney function.
3. **Adjust doses of renally cleared medications** based on current kidney function.
4. **Provide patient education on medication safety** and proper use.
5. **Monitor for drug-drug interactions** that could affect kidney function or medication efficacy.
6. **Support initiation and titration of guideline-directed therapies** such as ACE inhibitors, ARBs, SGLT2 inhibitors, and statins.
7. **Order labs and recommend therapy adjustments** under collaborative practice agreements.

### Social Workers
1. **Address psychosocial barriers to care** including depression, anxiety, and health literacy challenges.
2. **Connect patients to community resources** for social support needs.
3. **Assist with insurance navigation and medication access** to ensure continuous treatment.
4. **Provide counseling and support** for patients adjusting to CKD diagnosis and management.
