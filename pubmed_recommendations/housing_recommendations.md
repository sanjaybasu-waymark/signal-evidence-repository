# Housing Insecurity Recommendations

## Screening and Identification

1. **Implement standardized screening tools**:
   - Use validated housing insecurity screening questions in healthcare settings
   - Integrate screening into electronic health records
   - Conduct screening at intake, transitions of care, and when risk factors are present

2. **Adopt trauma-informed screening approaches**:
   - Train staff on culturally sensitive, non-stigmatizing screening methods
   - Create safe, private environments for screening
   - Use empathetic, person-centered language

3. **Establish systematic response protocols**:
   - Create clear workflows for positive screens
   - Develop standardized referral pathways
   - Document housing status in patient records

## Case Management Models

1. **Implement intensive case management**:
   - Provide comprehensive assessment and individualized care planning
   - Maintain lower caseloads (typically 15-20 clients per case manager)
   - Offer frequent, regular contact with clients
   - Coordinate across multiple service domains (healthcare, housing, benefits)

2. **Establish assertive community treatment (ACT) teams**:
   - Deploy multidisciplinary teams including nurses, social workers, and peer specialists
   - Provide 24/7 coverage for high-need individuals
   - Deliver services in community settings rather than offices
   - Focus on individuals with serious mental illness and high service needs

3. **Utilize critical time intervention (CTI)**:
   - Implement time-limited (typically 9-month) intensive support during transitions
   - Focus on housing stability during critical periods (hospital discharge, housing placement)
   - Gradually transfer care to ongoing community supports
   - Ensure continuity of care across systems

## Housing Interventions

1. **Adopt Housing First approaches**:
   - Provide immediate access to permanent housing without preconditions
   - Separate housing provision from service participation requirements
   - Offer optional supportive services based on individual needs and preferences
   - Focus on housing retention and stability

2. **Develop permanent supportive housing programs**:
   - Combine affordable housing with flexible, voluntary support services
   - Target individuals with chronic conditions, disabilities, or complex needs
   - Ensure long-term housing subsidies or affordable units
   - Integrate healthcare services with housing support

3. **Create medical respite/recuperative care programs**:
   - Establish safe discharge options for homeless patients leaving hospitals
   - Provide temporary housing with medical support for recovery
   - Connect patients to permanent housing during respite stay
   - Coordinate follow-up care and medication management

## Integrated Care Models

1. **Implement multidisciplinary care teams**:
   - Integrate primary care, mental health, substance use, and social services
   - Co-locate services in accessible locations
   - Develop shared care plans addressing medical and social needs
   - Include community health workers and peer specialists on teams

2. **Establish specialized healthcare for homeless programs**:
   - Create dedicated primary care services for people experiencing homelessness
   - Adapt care delivery to address unique needs and barriers
   - Offer extended hours, walk-in appointments, and mobile services
   - Provide trauma-informed, harm reduction approaches

3. **Integrate legal and financial services**:
   - Embed legal aid services within healthcare settings
   - Address housing discrimination, eviction prevention, and benefits access
   - Provide financial counseling and credit repair services
   - Assist with documentation and identification needs

## Role-Specific Recommendations

### Care Coordinators and Nurse Care Managers

1. **Screen for housing insecurity** using structured tools
2. **Develop individualized care plans** addressing housing needs
3. **Coordinate referrals to housing resources** and track outcomes
4. **Adjust medication regimens** to accommodate housing circumstances
5. **Facilitate communication** between healthcare and housing providers

### Community Health Workers (CHWs)

1. **Conduct outreach in shelters, encampments, and other locations**
2. **Build trust through consistent presence** and practical assistance
3. **Accompany clients to appointments** and help navigate systems
4. **Provide health education** adapted to living situations
5. **Connect clients to community resources** beyond healthcare

### Social Workers

1. **Conduct comprehensive psychosocial assessments**
2. **Provide case management** for complex housing situations
3. **Assist with applications** for housing programs and benefits
4. **Address multiple social determinants** affecting housing stability
5. **Advocate for clients** with landlords and housing authorities

### Pharmacists and Pharmacy Technicians

1. **Adapt medication regimens** for people without stable housing
2. **Provide medication storage solutions** when refrigeration is unavailable
3. **Synchronize medication refills** to reduce barriers to adherence
4. **Offer medication education** considering housing circumstances
5. **Coordinate with shelter staff** on medication administration when appropriate

## Implementation Strategies

1. **Develop community partnerships**:
   - Establish formal relationships with housing authorities
   - Create referral agreements with shelters and housing programs
   - Participate in local Continuums of Care
   - Engage with community organizations serving homeless populations

2. **Train healthcare staff**:
   - Provide education on housing insecurity impacts and interventions
   - Develop competencies for trauma-informed care
   - Address stigma and bias toward homeless patients
   - Include people with lived experience in training development

3. **Measure outcomes and impact**:
   - Track housing status over time
   - Monitor healthcare utilization and costs
   - Assess health outcomes for housed vs. unhoused patients
   - Evaluate patient experience and satisfaction
