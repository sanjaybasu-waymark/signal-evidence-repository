# Community Health Worker Best Practices Recommendations

## Extracted from: Best Practice Guidelines for Implementing and Evaluating Community Health Worker Programs in Health Care Settings & CDC Evidence of Effectiveness for Community Health Workers

Citations: 
1. Sinai Urban Health Institute. Best Practice Guidelines for Implementing and Evaluating Community Health Worker Programs in Health Care Settings. 2014.
2. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Evidence of Effectiveness for Community Health Workers. Heart Disease and Stroke Prevention Best Practices Clearinghouse. 2025.
3. National Council on Aging. How to Successfully Involve Community Health Workers in Delivering Evidence-Based Programming. 2024.

## Specific Bite-Sized Recommendations

### 1. Team-Based Care Integration
**Implementation Guidance:** Integrate CHWs into team-based care models by: 1) Including CHWs in regular care team meetings; 2) Establishing clear communication channels between CHWs and clinical staff; 3) Creating standardized documentation protocols for CHW encounters; 4) Developing referral pathways from clinical providers to CHWs; and 5) Ensuring CHWs have access to relevant patient information needed for their work.

**Evidence Level:** A - Strong
**Target Population:** Healthcare organizations serving populations with chronic conditions, particularly in underserved communities

### 2. Community-Based Recruitment Strategy
**Implementation Guidance:** Implement a community-based recruitment strategy for CHWs by: 1) Prioritizing candidates who are from and live in the target community; 2) Valuing lived experience and community knowledge as qualifications; 3) Involving community members in the interview process; 4) Assessing candidates' understanding of community needs and resources; and 5) Evaluating candidates' ability to build trust with community members.

**Evidence Level:** B - Moderate
**Target Population:** Organizations establishing or expanding CHW programs, particularly in communities with health disparities

### 3. Structured Chronic Disease Self-Management Support
**Implementation Guidance:** Train CHWs to deliver structured chronic disease self-management support through: 1) Teaching specific action planning techniques; 2) Demonstrating proper medication adherence strategies; 3) Providing tools for symptom monitoring; 4) Coaching on when and how to contact healthcare providers; and 5) Following up with patients at scheduled intervals based on condition severity.

**Evidence Level:** A - Strong
**Target Population:** Individuals with chronic conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, asthma, or heart failure

### 4. Social Determinants of Health Screening Protocol
**Implementation Guidance:** Implement a standardized social determinants of health screening protocol for CHWs that includes: 1) Validated screening questions for food insecurity, housing instability, transportation barriers, and utility needs; 2) A workflow for prioritizing identified needs; 3) A resource directory of community organizations addressing each domain; 4) Documentation templates for referrals made; and 5) A follow-up schedule to verify if needs were addressed.

**Evidence Level:** B - Moderate
**Target Population:** Individuals with complex health and social needs, particularly those in underserved communities

### 5. Cultural Concordance Matching
**Implementation Guidance:** When possible, match CHWs with clients from similar cultural backgrounds by: 1) Assessing language needs of the population served; 2) Recruiting CHWs who speak the languages prevalent in the community; 3) Training CHWs on culturally-specific health beliefs and practices; 4) Developing culturally tailored health education materials; and 5) Creating opportunities for CHWs to share cultural insights with the broader healthcare team.

**Evidence Level:** B - Moderate
**Target Population:** Communities with significant immigrant populations or cultural/linguistic minorities

### 6. Home Visitation Protocol
**Implementation Guidance:** Establish a structured home visitation protocol for CHWs that includes: 1) Pre-visit safety assessment; 2) Standardized home environment assessment checklist; 3) Specific health education topics to cover based on client needs; 4) Documentation of observations and interventions; and 5) Clear guidelines for when to escalate concerns to clinical team members.

**Evidence Level:** B - Moderate
**Target Population:** Individuals with limited mobility, complex health needs, or barriers to accessing facility-based care

### 7. Evidence-Based Program Leadership Training
**Implementation Guidance:** Train CHWs to lead evidence-based health promotion programs by: 1) Providing certification in specific evidence-based curricula (e.g., Chronic Disease Self-Management Program, Diabetes Prevention Program); 2) Offering practice teaching sessions with feedback; 3) Supplying standardized facilitation guides and materials; 4) Implementing fidelity monitoring through observation; and 5) Creating peer learning communities for ongoing support.

**Evidence Level:** A - Strong
**Target Population:** CHWs working in community settings with opportunities to deliver group-based interventions

### 8. Medication Adherence Support System
**Implementation Guidance:** Implement a structured medication adherence support system for CHWs that includes: 1) Training on common medications and their purposes; 2) Tools to assess barriers to medication adherence; 3) Practical strategies to address identified barriers (pill boxes, reminder systems); 4) Documentation templates to track adherence challenges; and 5) Clear protocols for when to alert clinical team members about adherence issues.

**Evidence Level:** B - Moderate
**Target Population:** Individuals with complex medication regimens, particularly older adults and those with multiple chronic conditions

### 9. Community Resource Navigation Protocol
**Implementation Guidance:** Develop a standardized community resource navigation protocol for CHWs that includes: 1) A regularly updated database of community resources; 2) Warm handoff procedures for connecting clients to resources; 3) Documentation templates for referrals made; 4) Follow-up timelines based on urgency of need; and 5) Feedback mechanisms to track the quality and accessibility of community resources.

**Evidence Level:** B - Moderate
**Target Population:** Individuals with unmet social needs that impact health outcomes

### 10. Health Literacy Assessment and Education
**Implementation Guidance:** Train CHWs to assess and address health literacy needs through: 1) Using validated health literacy screening tools; 2) Employing teach-back methods to confirm understanding; 3) Creating visual aids and simplified materials for complex health concepts; 4) Documenting preferred learning styles; and 5) Adapting education approaches based on literacy level.

**Evidence Level:** B - Moderate
**Target Population:** Individuals with limited health literacy, limited English proficiency, or limited formal education

### 11. Care Transition Support Protocol
**Implementation Guidance:** Implement a care transition support protocol for CHWs that includes: 1) Hospital/facility visit during admission when possible; 2) Pre-discharge planning participation; 3) Home visit within 48-72 hours post-discharge; 4) Medication reconciliation using structured tools; 5) Assessment of follow-up appointment understanding and transportation needs; and 6) Communication of findings to primary care team.

**Evidence Level:** A - Strong
**Target Population:** Individuals transitioning from hospital to home, particularly those at high risk for readmission

### 12. Motivational Interviewing Technique Application
**Implementation Guidance:** Train CHWs in specific motivational interviewing techniques including: 1) Open-ended questioning; 2) Affirmations of client strengths; 3) Reflective listening with specific response formats; 4) Summarizing client statements; and 5) Eliciting change talk through structured conversation guides. Provide practice scenarios specific to common health behaviors in the target population.

**Evidence Level:** B - Moderate
**Target Population:** Individuals needing support for health behavior change, particularly for chronic disease management

### 13. Professional Development Pathway
**Implementation Guidance:** Establish a clear professional development pathway for CHWs that includes: 1) Core competency training based on national standards; 2) Specialized training in health conditions prevalent in the community; 3) Opportunities for certification in evidence-based programs; 4) Regular skills assessment and feedback; and 5) Career advancement opportunities within the organization.

**Evidence Level:** C - Limited
**Target Population:** CHWs and organizations employing them, particularly in healthcare settings

### 14. Standardized Documentation System
**Implementation Guidance:** Implement a standardized documentation system for CHW activities that includes: 1) Templates for common encounter types; 2) Structured fields for social needs assessments; 3) Tracking of referrals made and their outcomes; 4) Integration with or communication to electronic health records when possible; and 5) Regular data review to identify patterns and needs.

**Evidence Level:** B - Moderate
**Target Population:** Organizations employing CHWs, particularly those seeking to evaluate program impact

### 15. Community Advisory Structure
**Implementation Guidance:** Establish a community advisory structure for CHW programs that includes: 1) Regular meetings with community members and leaders; 2) Mechanisms for community input on program priorities; 3) Participation of CHWs in advisory meetings; 4) Feedback channels for program improvement; and 5) Collaborative problem-solving for community health challenges.

**Evidence Level:** C - Limited
**Target Population:** Organizations implementing CHW programs, particularly in communities with historical distrust of healthcare systems
