# HIV Care Management Recommendations

## Multidisciplinary and Integrated Care Models

1. **Implement multidisciplinary team-based care** comprising physicians, pharmacists, nurses, social workers, mental health professionals, and patient navigators to improve medication adherence and retention in care.

2. **Establish integrated care or "medical home" models** that coordinate primary, specialty, and psychosocial services to reduce fragmentation and improve outcomes.

3. **Utilize comprehensive case management and care coordination** similar to Medicaid Target Case Management and Health Home programs to increase CD4 counts and care engagement, particularly among patients with complex needs.

## Evidence-Based Interventions

1. **Provide patient navigation services** to help patients access and navigate the healthcare system.

2. **Implement appointment reminder systems** to improve attendance at scheduled visits.

3. **Offer psychosocial support** to address mental health needs and improve engagement.

4. **Arrange transportation or appointment accompaniment** to overcome logistical barriers to care.

5. **Utilize data-to-care strategies** to identify and re-engage patients who have fallen out of care.

6. **Develop differentiated service delivery models** that tailor the location, intensity, frequency, and provider of services to individual patient needs.

7. **Initiate antiretroviral therapy (ART) rapidly** - ideally on the day of diagnosis or as soon as feasible - to reduce time to viral suppression and improve long-term retention.

## Addressing Social Determinants and Health Equity

1. **Provide housing assistance, food assistance, and transportation support** to address basic needs.

2. **Implement programs to address stigma and discrimination** that create barriers to care.

3. **Deliver culturally competent care** tailored to diverse populations.

4. **Conduct ongoing assessment of social and economic barriers** to identify and address obstacles to care.

5. **Involve people with HIV meaningfully in program design and implementation** to ensure relevance and acceptability.

6. **Perform intake and ongoing assessments** to identify and address social determinants of health.

## Innovative Care Delivery Approaches

1. **Utilize telehealth and mobile health platforms** to expand access and continuity of care, particularly for populations facing geographic or structural barriers.

2. **Address digital literacy and technology access issues** to ensure equitable benefit from digital health interventions.

## Comprehensive Approach

1. **Combine multidisciplinary, integrated care models with evidence-based strategies** such as patient navigation, rapid ART initiation, and differentiated service delivery.

2. **Systematically address social determinants of health** while leveraging innovative care delivery platforms.

3. **Target interventions to address disparities** among racial and ethnic minorities, transgender individuals, people who inject drugs, and those living in the southeastern United States.
