# Post-Stroke Care Management Recommendations

## Multidisciplinary and Integrated Care Models

1. **Implement team-based approaches** that include physicians, nurses, pharmacists, social workers, and rehabilitation specialists to improve risk factor control and secondary prevention.

2. **Establish regular hospital and outpatient programs** for adherence and risk factor monitoring using multidisciplinary teams to improve care quality and reduce recurrent stroke risk.

3. **Utilize case management-based programs** such as the SOS-Care model, which provide home and phone consultations, education, and individualized care plans to reduce stroke recurrence and vascular death.

4. **Implement the ABC integrated care pathway**:
   - **A**ppropriate antithrombotic therapy
   - **B**etter functional and psychological status
   - **C**ardiovascular risk factor and comorbidity optimization

## Risk Factor Management and Secondary Prevention

1. **Maintain strict hypertension control** with a target of <130/80 mm Hg for most patients after ischemic stroke, as recommended by the American Heart Association/American Stroke Association.

2. **Prescribe statin therapy** as indicated for secondary prevention.

3. **Provide antiplatelet or anticoagulation therapy** as indicated based on stroke etiology.

4. **Implement comprehensive lifestyle modification** including diet, exercise, and smoking cessation programs.

5. **Establish adherence monitoring systems** to track medication compliance and address barriers.

6. **Address social determinants of health** to improve outcomes and reduce disparities.

7. **Utilize nurse- and pharmacist-led interventions** for blood pressure and lipid management to achieve risk factor targets.

## Rehabilitation and Functional Recovery

1. **Initiate early, structured rehabilitation programs**, often community-based, to improve outcomes.

2. **Provide multimodal rehabilitation** including physical, occupational, and speech therapy to improve function, independence, and quality of life.

3. **Use validated screening tools** for mobility, cognition, and depression, with prompt linkage to appropriate resources.

4. **Implement community and home-based rehabilitation models**, including telerehabilitation, to increase access and engagement while maintaining quality outcomes.

## Patient and Caregiver Education, Engagement, and Support

1. **Deliver structured education programs** for patients and caregivers about stroke, risk factors, warning signs, and self-management strategies.

2. **Provide psychosocial support** to address depression, anxiety, and other mental health needs common after stroke.

3. **Facilitate social work involvement** to help address social and medical needs.

4. **Develop community resource networks** to facilitate transitions of care and support both patients and caregivers.

## Comprehensive Approach

1. **Combine multidisciplinary team-based care, case management, rigorous risk factor control, structured rehabilitation, and individualized education and support** as the specific care management and population health interventions that improve outcomes for patients after a stroke.
