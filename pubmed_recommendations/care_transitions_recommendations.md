# Care Transitions Management Recommendations

## Key Care Management Interventions for Care Transitions

### 1. Multidisciplinary, Bundled Transitional Care Models

1. **Implement Naylor or Coleman transitional care models** that employ advanced practice providers or transitions coaches to deliver a bundled approach including:
   - In-hospital engagement
   - Post-discharge home visits
   - Ongoing telephone support
   - Patient and caregiver empowerment
   - Self-management education
   - Medication reconciliation

2. **Establish structured follow-up protocols** to reduce 30-day hospital readmissions and healthcare costs.

### 2. Patient- and Family-Centered Interventions

1. **Incorporate comprehensive assessment components** including:
   - Health assessment
   - Medication reconciliation
   - Discharge planning
   - Symptom management
   - Emotional support

2. **Tailor interventions to patient and family needs** to achieve significant reductions in hospital readmissions.

3. **Increase the number of patient-centered components** to maximize benefits.

### 3. Complexity-Appropriate Transitional Care Interventions

1. **Implement low-complexity interventions** (structured discharge planning, brief follow-up) for reducing 30- and 180-day readmissions and ED visits.

2. **Utilize medium-complexity interventions** (adding medication management or more intensive follow-up) for 90-day effectiveness and improving medication adherence.

3. **Consider high-complexity interventions** primarily for improving patient satisfaction.

### 4. Intensive Programs for High-Need Patients

1. **Develop programs emphasizing rapid primary care follow-up** for high-need, high-cost Medicaid patients.

2. **Arrange intensive home visits** for vulnerable populations.

3. **Establish robust care coordination systems** to reduce 30-day readmissions, hospitalizations, ED visits, and overall medical expenditures.

### 5. Skilled Nursing Facility Transitions

1. **Implement structured, bi-directional communication** between hospital and SNF providers.

2. **Conduct regular interdisciplinary meetings** to eliminate hierarchy and foster shared learning environments.

3. **Use standardized transfer forms** for information exchange.

4. **Perform comprehensive medication reconciliation** during transitions.

5. **Establish clear accountability** for information transfer.

### 6. Transitions from Incarceration

1. **Align discharge planning with community standards**, including:
   - Provision of health records
   - Sufficient supply of medications
   - Patient education
   - Direct linkage to community-based care

2. **Implement the Transitions Clinic Network model** integrating community health workers with lived experience of incarceration into primary care teams.

## Role-Specific Interventions

### Care Coordinators and Nurse Care Managers

1. **Lead interdisciplinary predischarge planning** and ensure postdischarge follow-up.

2. **Maintain care continuity** with clear accountability for each transition step.

3. **Facilitate patient and caregiver engagement** through tailored education on:
   - Disease management
   - Warning signs
   - Self-care strategies

4. **Empower patients to participate in decision-making** about their care.

5. **Coordinate rapid primary care follow-up appointments** after discharge.

6. **Arrange home visits for high-need or high-risk patients** to ensure timely assessment and intervention.

### Community Health Workers (CHWs)

1. **Conduct home visits** to reinforce discharge instructions and assess home safety.

2. **Provide self-management education** including medication adherence and symptom monitoring.

3. **Support patients with transportation** to appointments.

4. **Accompany patients to primary care or specialty appointments** when needed.

5. **Assist with scheduling** to reduce barriers to care.

6. **For formerly incarcerated individuals**, integrate CHWs with lived experience of incarceration into primary care teams.

### Pharmacists and Pharmacy Technicians

1. **Perform comprehensive medication reconciliation** at all transition points.

2. **Identify and resolve medication discrepancies** before discharge.

3. **Provide medication education** to patients and caregivers.

4. **Ensure adequate medication supply** during transitions.

5. **Develop medication management plans** that account for patient-specific factors.

6. **Conduct post-discharge medication reviews** to identify and address adherence issues.

### Social Workers

1. **Assess social determinants of health** that may impact care transitions.

2. **Connect patients to community resources** for housing, food, transportation, and other needs.

3. **Facilitate insurance enrollment or continuation** during transitions.

4. **Provide emotional support and counseling** for patients and families.

5. **Coordinate with community-based organizations** to ensure continuity of social services.

6. **For incarcerated individuals**, establish linkages to community-based care and support services before release.
