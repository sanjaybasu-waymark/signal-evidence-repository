# Vaccination Adherence Recommendations

## Patient-Focused Interventions

1. **Implement patient reminder and recall systems** delivered via letters, text messages, phone calls, postcards, or electronic health record (EHR) messages to consistently increase immunization rates across age groups and settings.

2. **Provide tailored, culturally competent educational interventions** in plain language to enhance vaccine uptake, particularly in populations with low health literacy or limited access.

3. **Reduce out-of-pocket costs and provide financial incentives** to address cost barriers, especially effective in low socioeconomic status (SES) and adult populations.

## Provider and System-Level Interventions

1. **Utilize provider reminders through EHR prompts** to identify and prompt vaccination for eligible patients.

2. **Implement standing orders** for routine vaccination to standardize vaccine delivery.

3. **Conduct regular audit and feedback** to monitor vaccination rates and improve provider performance.

4. **Use immunization information systems** to track vaccination status and identify patients due for vaccines.

5. **Ensure strong provider recommendations** for vaccines, which is among the most effective behavioral interventions for increasing vaccine uptake.

6. **Identify vaccine champions within practices** to reinforce adherence and consistent messaging across care teams.

7. **Expand access through extended clinic hours, vaccine-only clinics, and on-site vaccination** in hospitals, emergency departments, or community settings.

## Role-Specific Interventions

### Care Coordinators
1. **Systematically review immunization status at every encounter**
2. **Use electronic health record (EHR) prompts** to identify patients due for vaccines
3. **Coordinate reminder/recall systems** via phone, text, or mail
4. **Facilitate scheduling and address barriers** such as transportation or cost
5. **Ensure documentation in immunization registries**

### Community Health Workers (CHWs)
1. **Conduct home visits** to reach patients who miss appointments
2. **Track and link defaulters** to vaccination services
3. **Provide culturally tailored education** about vaccines
4. **Update contact information** for hard-to-reach or marginalized populations
5. **Verify immunization records** and facilitate outreach in low-coverage communities

### Pharmacists
1. **Screen for vaccine eligibility** during all patient encounters
2. **Provide strong recommendations** for needed vaccines
3. **Administer vaccines under standing orders**
4. **Use motivational interviewing** to address vaccine hesitancy
5. **Ensure bidirectional communication with immunization registries**
6. **Collaborate with other providers** to reduce missed opportunities

### Pharmacy Technicians
1. **Identify eligible patients** for vaccination
2. **Prepare documentation** for vaccine administration
3. **Assist with patient education** about vaccines
4. **Administer vaccines** where permitted after accredited training

### Nurse Care Managers
1. **Assess immunization status** during routine care
2. **Deliver patient education** about vaccine benefits and safety
3. **Provide reminders** for upcoming or missed vaccinations
4. **Administer vaccines** during routine care visits
5. **Coordinate with primary care and public health teams** for follow-up

### Social Workers
1. **Identify and address social determinants** that impede vaccine access
2. **Provide tailored education** to address concerns
3. **Connect patients to community resources** or financial assistance programs
4. **Build trust** among vulnerable populations

## Community and Policy Approaches

1. **Support school-entry requirements** for vaccination.

2. **Implement community-based vaccination programs** in accessible locations.

3. **Develop partnerships with pharmacies and public health entities** to expand vaccination access.

4. **Engage communities affected by disparities** through tailored outreach, trust-building initiatives, and culturally relevant communication.

## Multicomponent Strategies

1. **Combine patient reminders, education, expanded access, provider/system changes, and community engagement** for the greatest improvements in vaccination adherence and equity.

2. **Address multiple barriers simultaneously** rather than using single-component approaches, particularly in marginalized or high-risk populations.
