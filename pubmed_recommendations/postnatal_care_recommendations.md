# Postnatal Care Management Recommendations

## Recommended Structure and Timing of Postnatal Care

1. **Implement ongoing, individualized care** rather than a single postpartum visit to improve outcomes.

2. **Schedule initial contact with an obstetric care provider** within the first 3 weeks postpartum, with a comprehensive assessment by 12 weeks.

3. **Establish coordinated care teams and clear transitions to primary care** ensuring continuity and addressing the long-term health implications of pregnancy complications.

## Home Visits and Follow-up

1. **Provide home visits by healthcare professionals or trained lay supporters** to improve maternal satisfaction, exclusive breastfeeding rates, and infant healthcare utilization.

2. **Implement individualized home-based care** to reduce postpartum depression scores and increase maternal satisfaction compared to facility-based care.

3. **Conduct early and frequent follow-up** with prompt office or home visits within 48-72 hours of hospital discharge and additional contacts as needed to facilitate early detection of complications and support maternal-infant bonding.

## Comprehensive Assessment and Support

1. **Perform comprehensive biopsychosocial assessments** including screening for postpartum depression, intimate partner violence, and management of chronic diseases.

2. **Prioritize early identification and intervention for mental health and social risks** as essential for optimizing maternal outcomes.

3. **Provide breastfeeding support through peer support programs and lactation consultants** to increase breastfeeding initiation and duration.

4. **Offer contraception at the comprehensive postpartum visit, or earlier if desired** to prevent unintended pregnancy and support optimal birth spacing.

## Lifestyle and Risk Management

1. **Implement lifestyle and cardiovascular risk interventions** for individuals with adverse pregnancy outcomes (APOs) such as gestational diabetes or hypertensive disorders.

2. **Refer to structured lifestyle programs** (e.g., the National Diabetes Prevention Program), digital health tools, and community-based interventions to reduce postpartum weight retention and improve cardiovascular health.

3. **Recommend 150 minutes of moderate-intensity exercise per week postpartum** with gradual resumption or initiation of activity as appropriate.

4. **Use reminders and technology** (e.g., automated reminders for glucose tolerance testing) to improve adherence to recommended postpartum screenings, particularly for women with a history of gestational diabetes or preeclampsia.
