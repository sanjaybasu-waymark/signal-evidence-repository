# Substance Use Disorder Recommendations

## Screening and Identification

1. **Implement SBIRT (Screening, Brief Intervention, and Referral to Treatment)**:
   - Use validated screening tools (e.g., single-question screens, DAST, AUDIT) in primary care
   - Integrate brief motivational counseling for positive screens
   - Establish streamlined referral pathways to treatment
   - Train all staff in non-stigmatizing screening approaches

2. **Develop systematic identification protocols**:
   - Create registry systems to track patients with SUDs
   - Implement regular rescreening intervals
   - Use EHR alerts and decision support tools
   - Establish clear workflows for positive screens

## Case Management and Care Coordination

1. **Implement structured case management**:
   - Provide comprehensive assessment and individualized care planning
   - Facilitate service linkage across medical, behavioral, and social services
   - Conduct ongoing monitoring and outreach
   - Focus on improving treatment linkage and retention

2. **Establish chronic care management models**:
   - Incorporate motivational interviewing techniques
   - Provide relapse prevention counseling
   - Coordinate medical and psychiatric care
   - Adapt approaches for specific substances (e.g., alcohol dependence)

3. **Develop robust outreach strategies**:
   - Implement flexible service delivery options
   - Use multiple contact methods (phone, text, in-person)
   - Provide engagement incentives
   - Address barriers to care (transportation, childcare)

## Behavioral and Psychosocial Interventions

1. **Implement contingency management programs**:
   - Provide tangible incentives contingent on objective evidence of behavior change
   - Use higher-value incentives for maximum efficacy
   - Minimize delay between behavior and reward
   - Extend intervention duration for sustained effects

2. **Offer evidence-based behavioral therapies**:
   - Cognitive behavioral therapy
   - Motivational enhancement therapy
   - 12-step facilitation
   - Community reinforcement approach
   - Facilitate mutual help group involvement

## Integrated and Primary Care-Based Models

1. **Establish multidisciplinary care teams for medication-assisted treatment (MAT)**:
   - Integrate buprenorphine or methadone treatment in primary care
   - Include care managers (nurses or pharmacists)
   - Develop patient agreements and protocols
   - Create flexible induction procedures

2. **Implement continuing care models**:
   - Provide longitudinal, adaptive follow-up
   - Conduct regular assessment and treatment adjustment
   - Enhance with digital/mobile health interventions
   - Target high-risk patients for intensive support

## Role-Specific Recommendations

### Care Coordinators

1. **Coordinate multidisciplinary care**:
   - Facilitate communication between providers
   - Ensure comprehensive treatment planning
   - Track patient progress across services

2. **Facilitate referrals to SUD treatment**:
   - Develop relationships with treatment providers
   - Create warm handoff protocols
   - Follow up on referral completion

3. **Ensure follow-up after transitions**:
   - Contact patients after hospital discharge
   - Coordinate post-detox care
   - Facilitate smooth transitions between levels of care

4. **Address barriers to care**:
   - Assist with transportation arrangements
   - Connect to housing resources
   - Help navigate insurance issues

### Community Health Workers

1. **Deliver psychoeducation and motivational enhancement**:
   - Provide substance use education
   - Offer personalized feedback
   - Use motivational interviewing techniques

2. **Support engagement through outreach**:
   - Meet patients in community settings
   - Maintain regular contact
   - Build trust through consistent presence

3. **Help address social determinants of health**:
   - Connect to community resources
   - Assist with basic needs
   - Support family engagement

### Pharmacists

1. **Support medication for opioid use disorder (MOUD)**:
   - Collaborate with prescribers for buprenorphine or methadone
   - Provide medication education
   - Monitor for drug interactions
   - Ensure appropriate dosing

2. **Implement harm reduction strategies**:
   - Distribute naloxone and provide training
   - Offer syringe access when appropriate
   - Educate on safer use practices

3. **Monitor medication adherence**:
   - Track prescription fills
   - Identify patterns suggesting misuse
   - Communicate concerns to treatment team

### Pharmacy Technicians

1. **Serve as frontline contacts**:
   - Identify patients at risk
   - Provide nonjudgmental support
   - Facilitate communication with pharmacists

2. **Recognize potential misuse**:
   - Monitor for early refill requests
   - Identify concerning patterns
   - Alert pharmacists appropriately

3. **Ensure appropriate counseling**:
   - Flag prescriptions requiring pharmacist consultation
   - Maintain patient privacy during discussions
   - Connect patients to resources

### Nurse Care Managers

1. **Lead care management for MOUD in primary care**:
   - Conduct initial and ongoing assessments
   - Coordinate with prescribers
   - Monitor treatment adherence
   - Provide patient education and support

2. **Implement the Massachusetts nurse care manager model**:
   - Serve as the main point of contact for patients
   - Facilitate buprenorphine induction
   - Provide regular follow-up
   - Coordinate care team communication

### Social Workers

1. **Conduct comprehensive SUD assessment**:
   - Evaluate substance use patterns
   - Assess psychosocial needs
   - Identify co-occurring disorders
   - Determine appropriate level of care

2. **Connect patients to resources**:
   - Link to behavioral health services
   - Assist with social service applications
   - Provide case management
   - Address housing and financial needs

3. **Advocate for integrated care**:
   - Support structural changes to improve access
   - Address stigma within healthcare systems
   - Promote continuity of care

## Implementation Strategies

1. **Develop integrated care models**:
   - Co-locate addiction services with primary care
   - Create shared treatment plans
   - Implement regular team communication
   - Use registry tracking systems

2. **Leverage technology**:
   - Implement digital/mobile health interventions
   - Use telehealth to expand access
   - Develop patient portals and apps
   - Employ automated monitoring systems

3. **Address workforce needs**:
   - Provide training on evidence-based practices
   - Implement team-based care models
   - Address stigma and burnout
   - Create supervision and support structures
