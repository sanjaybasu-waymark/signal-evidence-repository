About the Signal Evidence Library

A comprehensive, evidence-based library with real recommendations across multiple clinical domains and healthcare roles.

Project Overview

The Signal Evidence Library is a comprehensive repository of evidence-based healthcare recommendations designed to support population health care management teams. Our library contains hundreds of real, clinically validated recommendations across multiple domains and healthcare roles.

Each recommendation is backed by recent peer-reviewed literature, with a focus on publications from 2020-2024, and includes specific implementation guidance and expected outcomes with metrics.

Evidence Levels

We classify recommendations by evidence strength:

  • Level A: Strong evidence from multiple high-quality RCTs or systematic reviews
  • Level B: Moderate evidence from well-designed studies
  • Level C: Limited evidence from observational studies or expert opinion

Content Quality Standards

All recommendations in our library adhere to strict quality standards:

  • Action-oriented: Using specific verbs (conduct, provide, coordinate, implement)
  • Evidence-based: Citing peer-reviewed literature from the last 5 years
  • Measurable outcomes: Including specific percentages and metrics
  • Implementable: Providing clear, actionable guidance
